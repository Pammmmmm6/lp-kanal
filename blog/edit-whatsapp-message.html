How to edit a WhatsApp message (15-minute limit)

You can edit a WhatsApp message for 15 minutes after sending it. The steps on Android, iPhone and Web, what the other person sees, and what to do after.

Nicolas ProvostOct 7, 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • You can edit a WhatsApp message for 15 minutes after sending it by pressing and holding the message, tapping Edit, changing the text and confirming.
  • The edited message shows the word "edited" next to its timestamp for everyone in the chat, with no new notification and no visible edit history.
  • Photos, videos and other media cannot be edited, and after 15 minutes the remaining option is Delete for Everyone, available for two days.

You can edit a WhatsApp message for up to 15 minutes after sending it: press and hold the message, tap Edit (on Android, tap the three dots first), change the text and confirm. The message updates for everyone in the chat and shows the word "edited" next to its timestamp.

Those rules come from WhatsApp's Help Center page How to edit messages, read on October 7, 2026. This guide covers the steps on each device, what the other person sees, what cannot be edited, and what to do once the 15 minutes have passed.

How long do you have to edit a WhatsApp message?

You have 15 minutes from the moment you send the message. WhatsApp's Help Center page is explicit, as of October 7, 2026: there is a 15 minute time limit for editing messages. After that, the message can no longer be edited.

Once the window has closed, you have two documented options:

What does the other person see when you edit a message?

Here is what WhatsApp documents:

  • A label. The edited message carries the word "edited" next to its timestamp, visible to everyone in the chat.
  • No new notification. Editing a message does not send a new chat notification to the people in the chat.
  • No edit history. When it announced the feature on its blog on May 22, 2023, WhatsApp said the label lets people know about the correction without showing the edit history.
  • Read receipts start again. According to WhatsApp's page on read receipts, read on October 7, 2026, read receipts reset when you edit a message, so you can see who viewed the updated version. Our guide to WhatsApp ticks explains how to read them.
  • Older versions of the app. Someone who is not on the latest version of WhatsApp sees a notice saying the message was edited for everyone on the latest version, and has to update the app to see edited messages.

One limit follows from all this: an edit replaces the text in the chat, but it cannot undo the fact that someone already read the first version.

What can't you edit on WhatsApp?

WhatsApp's page lists two limits:

  • Messages sent more than 15 minutes ago.
  • Photos, videos and other types of media. If the wrong file went out, deleting the message for everyone and sending the right one is the documented way out.

Why is there no Edit option on my message?

Start with the two limits above: the message may be older than 15 minutes, or it may be a photo, a video or another type of media. Those are the limits WhatsApp's page gives.

If neither applies, update WhatsApp. The same Help Center page asks people who are not on the latest version to update the app in order to see edited messages, so an outdated app is the first thing to rule out.

Can you edit a WhatsApp channel update?

Channels follow a different rule. According to WhatsApp's page How to edit channel updates, read on October 7, 2026, a channel admin can edit some updates for up to 30 days. The steps on Android mirror those of a chat: press and hold the update, tap the three dots, select Edit, make the change and tap the check mark.

The edited update shows the word "edited" next to its timestamp, and followers are not notified. Photos, videos, audio updates, polls, other media and forwarded updates cannot be edited.

Are edited messages still end-to-end encrypted?

Yes. In its announcement of May 22, 2023, WhatsApp states that messages and the edits made to them are protected by end-to-end encryption, like all personal messages, media and calls.

Edit, delete or let it disappear: which one should you use?

  • A typo spotted right away: edit the message, within 15 minutes.
  • A message sent to the wrong chat: delete it for everyone. WhatsApp's Help Center adds that a deleted message cannot be brought back unless it was included in a backup, a case covered in our guide to recovering deleted WhatsApp messages.
  • A conversation you do not want to keep: turn on disappearing messages, which erase new messages after the duration you choose.
  • A conversation you want to keep away from prying eyes: use chat lock.

Frequently asked questions

How do I edit a WhatsApp message after sending it?

Press and hold the message, tap Edit, change the text and confirm with the check mark. On Android, tap the three dots after holding the message, then select Edit. On WhatsApp Web, hover over the message, click the menu arrow and choose Edit message. According to WhatsApp's Help Center, read on October 7, 2026, this works for 15 minutes after sending.

How long do I have to edit a WhatsApp message?

You have 15 minutes after sending. WhatsApp's Help Center, read on October 7, 2026, states that there is a 15 minute time limit for editing messages. Once it has passed, the message can no longer be changed. You can still delete it for everyone, which the same Help Center allows for two days after sending, and send a corrected version.

Does the other person know I edited a message?

Yes. An edited message shows the word "edited" next to its timestamp, for everyone in the chat. WhatsApp does not send a new notification when you edit, and its announcement of the feature says the edit history is not shown. Someone who read the first version before your correction has still seen it.

Can I edit a photo or a video sent on WhatsApp?

No. WhatsApp's Help Center, read on October 7, 2026, says you can't edit photos, videos or other types of media. Editing applies to the text of a message you sent, for 15 minutes. If the wrong photo went out, the documented option is to delete the message for everyone and send the right one.

What can I do once the 15 minutes have passed?

The message can no longer be edited. WhatsApp's Help Center, read on October 7, 2026, gives you two days after sending to request Delete for Everyone, which replaces the message with "This message was deleted". You can then send a corrected message. Past both limits, the simplest fix is a short follow-up message that sets things straight.
