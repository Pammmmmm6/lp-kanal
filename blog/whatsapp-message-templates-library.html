50+ WhatsApp Message Templates for Ecommerce: Copy and Paste (2026)

53 WhatsApp message templates ready to copy and paste: abandoned cart, order updates, review requests, winback and promos. With Meta categories.

Nicolas ProvostAug 7, 2026 · 19 min read

Key takeaways

  • The library holds 53 templates in 10 categories, from welcome and abandoned cart to shipping, reviews, winback and loyalty, each with its Meta category.
  • Every business-initiated message needs a template approved by Meta, so submit early and allow 7 to 10 days before Black Friday.
  • In a cart sequence, send a friendly nudge after 1 hour, social proof after 24 hours and keep the discount for the third reminder.

Quick answer

This page is a library of 53 WhatsApp message templates for ecommerce, organized in 10 categories: welcome, abandoned cart, order confirmation, shipping, reviews, winback, back in stock, promotions, customer service, and loyalty. Copy any template, replace the {{1}}-style variables with your own data, and submit it to Meta for approval before sending: every business-initiated WhatsApp message must use an approved template. For the rules behind approval, categories and pricing, read our WhatsApp Business templates guide; this page is the copy-paste companion.

Template rules in 30 seconds

Four things to know before you copy anything below. The full theory lives in the templates guide and the WhatsApp Business API guide, so here is just the survival kit:

  • Categories matter. Meta classifies every template as Marketing, Utility or Authentication (see the official template documentation), and prices them differently. Promotional content submitted as Utility gets rejected or reclassified, so each template below states its correct category.
  • Variables personalize. The {{1}}, {{2}} placeholders are filled per recipient at send time (name, product, order number, discount code). Compare the reply rate of a personalized message with a generic blast on your own list.
  • Approval first. Submit templates to Meta before you need them. Meta says a review can take up to 24 hours.
  • Keep it human. Aim for 2 to 4 sentences that sound like a person and end with one clear call to action. Discounts stay generic here on purpose: fill the discount variables with values that match your margin, not ours.

1. Welcome and opt-in messages

The first message sets the tone for every message after it. If you want 50 more openers sorted by industry, we keep a dedicated library of WhatsApp greeting message examples.

Template 1: Opt-in confirmation

Hi {{1}}, you're in ✅ This is the official {{2}} WhatsApp line: order updates, early access and the occasional good deal. Save this number so our messages never get lost.

Category: Utility · Tip: Send within a minute of signup. Stating what you will send (and how often) up front protects your quality rating later.

Template 2: Welcome with brand introduction

Welcome to {{1}}, {{2}} 👋 We make {{3}} and ship every order within 48 hours. Looking for something specific? Reply here, a real human reads every message.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Inviting a reply opens the 24-hour service window and signals to Meta that your conversations are wanted.

Template 3: Welcome offer

Hi {{1}}, thanks for joining {{2}} on WhatsApp 🎁 Your welcome gift: {{3}} off your first order with code {{4}}, valid 7 days. Reply STOP anytime to opt out.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Pair with a "Shop now" URL button. Keep the validity honest: if the code says 7 days, it expires in 7 days.

Template 4: VIP early-access welcome

Hi {{1}}, you made the {{2}} VIP list ⭐ From now on you hear about drops, restocks and sales 24 hours before everyone else. First perk lands this week.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Only promise early access if your send calendar actually delivers it. VIP segments should be built from engaged clickers.

Template 5: Preference capture

Quick question, {{1}}: what should we send you from {{2}}? Reply 1 for {{3}}, 2 for {{4}}, 3 for both. We'll only message you about what you pick.

Category: Marketing · Tip: A numbered reply takes less effort than an open question, and the answers segment your list from day one, so future campaigns can match what each contact picked.

2. Abandoned cart messages (3-reminder sequence included)

Cart recovery is a good first automation on WhatsApp: the shopper already picked the product. Templates 6 to 8 form a ready-to-ship sequence; for 15 more variants, see the abandoned cart recovery templates, and to automate the trigger from Shopify, the abandoned cart recovery feature does it end to end.

Template 6: Reminder 1, the friendly nudge (send after 1 hour)

Hi {{1}}, your {{2}} is still in your cart at {{3}} 🛒 We saved everything, checkout takes under a minute. Anything holding you back? Reply here and we'll help.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Send 30 to 60 minutes after abandonment with a "Complete my order" button to the pre-filled cart. No discount yet.

Template 7: Reminder 2, social proof (send after 24 hours)

Still thinking it over, {{1}}? Fair enough. The {{2}} averages {{3}} stars from {{4}} reviews, and customers keep mentioning {{5}}. Your cart is saved whenever you're ready.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Answer the real objection (fit, quality, shipping cost) instead of repeating reminder 1. Reviews do the selling for you.

Template 8: Reminder 3, the incentive (send after 48 hours)

Last nudge, {{1}}, promise. Your cart at {{2}} expires soon, so here's {{3}} off with code {{4}} if you order today. Reply STOP if you'd rather we didn't follow up.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Hold the discount until message 3: some shoppers only need a reminder, so leading with a code just burns margin.

Template 9: High-value cart, concierge touch

Hi {{1}}, this is {{2}} from {{3}}. I noticed you were looking at the {{4}} and wanted to check in personally: any questions on sizing, materials or delivery? Happy to help before you decide.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Reserve for carts above twice your average order value. A message from a named person reads like a conversation, not a campaign.

Template 10: Low-stock cart alert

Heads up, {{1}}: the {{2}} in your cart is down to the last {{3}} units in your size. No pressure, we just didn't want you to find it gone tomorrow.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Send only when inventory is genuinely low. Fabricated scarcity gets reported as spam and drags down your quality rating.

Template 11: Failed payment recovery

Category: Utility · Tip: Send within 5 minutes. A failed payment is proven intent, not abandonment: track how many of those orders the template brings back.

Template 12: Standard order confirmation

Category: Utility · Tip: Always state the next step and when it happens. It answers the "where is my order" question before it turns into a ticket.

Template 13: Confirmation with delivery estimate

Category: Utility · Tip: Date ranges beat exact dates: promise the wider window, deliver early, collect the goodwill.

Template 14: Preorder confirmation

Category: Utility · Tip: Preorder customers check in more often, and one proactive date answers their main question before they ask it.

Template 15: Address confirmation

Category: Utility · Tip: Use for high-value orders and any address that fails validation. A failed delivery means return shipping, a reshipment and a support conversation: weigh that against the price of one utility message.

Template 16: Cash on delivery confirmation

Category: Utility · Tip: In COD-heavy markets this confirmation filters out orders that would be refused at the door: compare your refusal rate before and after. Automate the cancellation for silence.

Category: Marketing · Tip: Consumables only, timed from actual purchase cycles in your order history. Relevance here reads as service, not selling.

Frequently asked questions

What is a WhatsApp message template?

A WhatsApp message template is a pre-approved message format required to start a conversation with a customer through the WhatsApp Business API. You submit the text once to Meta, with numbered variables like {{1}} for the personalized parts, and once approved you can send it at scale: campaigns, cart reminders, shipping updates. Inside the 24-hour window that follows a customer reply, free-form messages need no template.

How long does Meta take to approve a WhatsApp template?

Meta says a template review can take up to 24 hours. Plan ahead for peak season: submit Black Friday templates 7 to 10 days early, because review queues slow down when every brand submits at once.

Can I personalize WhatsApp message templates?

Yes. Templates support numbered variables such as {{1}} and {{2}} that are filled per recipient at send time: first name, product, order number, discount code, delivery date. With a Shopify integration like Kanal, variables are filled automatically from store data, so every message reads like it was written for one person. Test a personalized version against a generic one on reply and conversion rates.

Why do WhatsApp templates get rejected?

Templates get rejected for reasons such as a category mismatch (promotional content submitted as utility), malformed or missing variables, URL shorteners like bit.ly, spammy formatting (all caps, emoji walls), and misleading claims or fake urgency. Fix the flagged issue and resubmit, or appeal from Business Manager. Clean grammar, honest offers and a visible opt-out path leave the reviewer nothing to flag.
