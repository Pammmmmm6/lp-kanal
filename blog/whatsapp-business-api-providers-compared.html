What Is a WhatsApp Business API Provider (BSP)?

A WhatsApp Business API provider, often called a Business Solution Provider (BSP), is a company that gives businesses access to the WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta's developer documentation now calls them Solution Partners: Meta Business Partners that provide messaging services, billing, integration support and customer support. Tech Providers offer the same services on their own or with a Solution Partner. You can browse them in Meta's official partner directory. If you're new to the BSP ecosystem, start with our complete WhatsApp Business API guide before picking a provider.

The WhatsApp Business app works from a phone. Once you need automation, several agents or a connection to your store, you need the API, and a provider saves you from building the tooling yourself. Some providers sell raw API access, others a complete marketing product. The right pick depends on what you sell and who runs the channel. If you are comparing marketing tools rather than API access, read our comparison of WhatsApp marketing software.

You can also use the API without a provider. Meta lets you connect to its Cloud API yourself: you register as a Meta developer, create an app with the WhatsApp use case, connect a WhatsApp Business account and generate an access token. You then build the inbox, template management, automations and analytics yourself. That layer is what providers sell.

  • Meta fees: how Meta's message fees are billed: passed through, charged at the provider's own rate, or billed by Meta directly.
  • E-commerce depth: Shopify integration, abandoned cart recovery, order events.
  • Channels: WhatsApp only, or WhatsApp next to SMS, email, Instagram and others.
  • Hosting and compliance: what the provider states publicly about GDPR and data location.
  • Getting started: app install, self-serve trial or sales-led onboarding.

Which platforms help with templates, approvals and compliance?

Every template is reviewed by Meta, whichever provider submits it, and Meta decides its category. So no provider approves templates faster on its own authority. Providers differ on the tooling around submission:

  • Template submission from the product: Wati includes template submission on all plans. Bird's WhatsApp API covers templates, buttons and carousels. Kanal's premium setup covers template writing and Meta validation.
  • Template versions at scale: none of the pages we read documents version control for templates. If you manage many templates across markets, ask the vendor how versions, languages and categories are tracked, and whether templates can be exported if you migrate.
  • Opt-in and consent: Kanal states it is fully GDPR-compliant: customers opt in to marketing messages, can opt out at any time, and consent is managed automatically.
  • Data location: Sinch's Conversation API offers hosting in the US, the EU (Ireland) or Brazil. 360dialog is a German company, Charles is registered in Berlin, and Trengo hosts its application on AWS. For the others, ask for the hosting region and the data processing agreement in writing.

Twilio is a programmable communications platform: WhatsApp runs through its APIs next to SMS, RCS, voice and email, at $0.005 per message plus Meta's fees, and your developers build the flows. 360dialog sells WhatsApp API access per number. The alternative depends on what you want instead:

How do you switch WhatsApp API providers without losing your number?

If your current provider is holding you back (unclear pricing, missing features, reliability), you can move your number to another one. Switching is a real operation, not a click. Meta documents it as a phone number migration between WhatsApp Business Accounts, in its migration guide.

What moves with your number

  • Display name, quality rating and messaging limits.
  • Official Business Account status.
  • Approved, high-quality templates, which Meta duplicates to the new account.
  • Registration is instant, so you keep sending and receiving messages during the move.

What you redo

  • Templates: you cannot use them until they are duplicated. Meta re-checks their category and may reject some, and template quality ratings restart at unknown, with a new rating after the first 24 hours if there is enough data.
  • Webhooks and integrations: point them to the new provider so incoming messages and store events keep flowing.
  • Contacts and automations: import or rebuild them in the new tool.

Before you start

  • Your Meta business must be verified.
  • Your current WhatsApp Business Account must be approved.
  • Two-step verification must be disabled on the number.

Provider notes

  • Moving to Kanal: Kanal walks you through number setup during onboarding, with a new number or an existing one that is not tied to a personal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app account.
  • Wati: its dedicated onboarding service (one-time fee) includes BSP migration.
  • API-first setups (Twilio, 360dialog, Sinch Conversation API): plan developer time to reconnect webhooks and custom code to the new provider.

Common pitfalls during any migration

  • Scheduling campaigns before templates are duplicated: they stay unusable until the migration is done.
  • Forgetting webhook URLs: incoming messages never reach your new tool.
  • Creating a new business portfolio instead of migrating: new portfolios start with a messaging limit of 250, shared by all numbers in the portfolio, per Meta's messaging limits. Our quality rating guide explains how to protect your sending capacity.
  • Customer expectations: nothing changes for your customers, who keep writing to the same number.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp Business API provider in 2026?

It depends on what you run on WhatsApp. For an online store on Shopify, our pick is Kanal, a WhatsApp marketing and sales app that installs from the Shopify App Store and syncs products, carts and orders. For developers, Twilio and Sinch sell programmable messaging APIs and 360dialog sells API access per number. Trengo, Wati and Respond.io center on a shared team inbox.

What is a WhatsApp BSP?

A WhatsApp BSP (Business Solution Provider) is a company that gives other businesses access to the WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta's developer documentation now calls them Solution Partners: Meta Business Partners that provide messaging services, billing, integration support and customer support. A business can also connect to Meta's Cloud API directly and build the inbox, templates and automations itself.

How much does a WhatsApp Business API provider cost?

You pay two layers: Meta's per-message fees and the provider's own price. Published examples read on October 8, 2026: Twilio charges $0.005 per message on top of Meta's fees, Gupshup $0.001 per message, 360dialog €49 per number per month, Wati starts at €59 per month billed annually and Trengo at €299. Kanal passes WhatsApp message costs through at cost.

Which WhatsApp API provider suits high-volume messaging?

Sending capacity is set by Meta's messaging limits, which apply whichever provider you use. Providers differ on what surrounds them: Twilio, Sinch, Bird, 360dialog and Infobip expose APIs and webhooks for teams that build their own stack, and Respond.io says it is built for businesses running high-volume WhatsApp conversations. At volume, compare a per-message fee, a per-number fee and a plan sized on contacts.

Can I switch WhatsApp API providers without losing my number?

Yes. Meta lets you migrate a business phone number from one WhatsApp Business Account to another. According to Meta, the number keeps its display name, quality rating, messaging limits and Official Business Account status, and you keep sending and receiving messages. Templates are duplicated and re-checked, so some can be rejected. Two-step verification must be disabled on the number first.

Which WhatsApp API provider should I choose in the US, Germany, APAC or LATAM?

Meta's message fees depend on the recipient's country, not on the provider, so start with the rates for your markets.
