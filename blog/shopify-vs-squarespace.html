Shopify vs Squarespace: Pricing, Fees and Selling (2026)

Shopify vs Squarespace compared for US merchants: plan prices, card rates, transaction fees, checkout, apps, support and how to move from one to the other.

Nicolas ProvostOct 7, 2026 · 13 min read

Key takeaways

  • Shopify is a commerce platform built around the store and checkout, while Squarespace is a website builder whose plans all include selling tools.
  • Squarespace Basic costs $25 a month against $39 for Shopify Basic, but adds a 2% commerce transaction fee that disappears from Core up.
  • Published US card rates match at 2.9% + 30¢ on entry plans, so from Squarespace Core up the choice rests on the product.

Shopify is a commerce platform built around the store, the checkout and an app marketplace, while Squarespace is a website builder whose plans all include selling tools alongside pages, portfolios, scheduling and memberships. For a US merchant on October 7, 2026, Shopify Basic costs $39 a month ($29 paid yearly) with 2.9% + 30¢ per online card payment, and Squarespace Basic costs $25 a month ($19 paid annually) with 2.9% + $0.30 per card payment plus a 2% commerce transaction fee that disappears from the Core plan up.

Everything below comes from each company's own pages, read on October 7, 2026: Shopify's pricing page, with the prices shown to US stores, and its Help Center, and Squarespace's pricing page, with the prices shown to US visitors, and its Help Center. Prices and fees can change: check both pages before you commit.

What is the difference between Shopify and Squarespace?

Shopify starts from the store. Its pricing page lists what every plan includes: unlimited products, themes, Shopify's checkout, selling on social media and marketplaces, discount codes, gift cards and abandoned checkout recovery, with hosting and an SSL certificate. Content pages and a blog exist, but the product, the order and the checkout are the center of the admin.

Squarespace starts from the website. Its Help Center guide to choosing a plan describes a drag-and-drop editing system to lay out content, galleries of images or videos and navigation menus, and adds that you can invoice clients and sell products, content and memberships on any plan. The same guide presents Basic as well suited to smaller sites with one or two contributors, and Advanced as a good option for larger e-commerce stores that process many orders.

Both let you sell an unlimited number of products. The difference is where each product puts its effort, and you can see it in the price list.

What do they say about payments and checkout?

Shopify. Shopify Payments is Shopify's own provider, and its rates fall as the plan rises. In the United States you offer PayPal through PayPal Wallet inside Shopify Payments, at rates starting at 3.49% + 49¢. Every plan uses Shopify's checkout, and checkout customization is limited on Basic, Grow and Advanced and full on Plus, the plan covered in our guide to Shopify Plus pricing.

Squarespace. Squarespace Payments is described as its native payment solution, fully integrated within the platform. The same Help Center guide says you can also connect a third-party processor such as Stripe, PayPal or Square, with Square available for Squarespace point of sale in the iOS app only. Its US table lists Klarna at 5.45% + $0.30 and Afterpay at 6% + $0.30. For the checkout page, the guide to checkout on your domain explains that with a custom domain and SSL set to Secure, shoppers stay on a checkout URL that carries your domain, and that checkout on all plans is protected by SSL and is PCI compliant.

Both platforms state that abandoned checkout recovery is included from the entry plan.

How do apps and extensions compare?

The Shopify App Store lists over 16,000 apps. Each developer sets its price, and most charges appear on your Shopify bill. Our best Shopify apps comparisons group them by need.

Squarespace describes Squarespace Extensions as a collection of third-party tools, the equivalent of plugins, add-ons or apps, for tasks like sales tax compliance, drop shipping, SEO and social media marketing. You can connect them on all plans at no additional cost through Squarespace, though some services require their own subscriptions. Squarespace adds that extensions fall outside its scope of support, beyond connecting and disconnecting them. Custom code (JavaScript and iframes in code blocks, code injection) is listed among the premium features of Core and higher plans.

If a specific tool matters to your business, check where it runs before you choose. One example from our side: Kanal, the WhatsApp marketing and sales app we build, is a Shopify app, installed from the Shopify App Store.

Where can you sell beyond your own site?

Shopify's pricing page lists selling on social media, on marketplaces and in AI chats in every plan. For marketplaces connected to Shopify, the first 50 synced orders each month are free, then a 1% fee applies, capped at $99 a month. In-person selling is included for pop-ups and events, and POS Pro costs $89 a month per location.

Squarespace's plan guide lists Squarespace point of sale and syncing products with Facebook and Instagram from the Basic plan, and Google shopping from Core.

Who owns the customer relationship and the data?

On both platforms, the customers are yours.

  • Shopify: its Terms of Service state that the services are not a marketplace, that any contract of sale is directly between you and the customer, and that you are the seller or merchant of record.
  • Squarespace: customer contacts are created automatically after a customer checks out, with order history, email address and total spent, and you are responsible for helping customers with their accounts. The same page notes that it's not possible to create, import or edit customers manually at this time. Products export to a .csv file, up to 10,000 products, without customer reviews, featured images or variant images.

What support does each offer?

Shopify's pricing page lists 24/7 live chat support on Basic and Grow, enhanced live chat on Advanced and priority phone support on Plus.

Squarespace's Help Center says it offers support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through email, live chat, X, Facebook Messenger and its mobile app, with live chat in English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. It does not offer assistance by phone, and does not provide support for custom code, SEO strategy, tax advice or third-party services.

Can you use both, or move from one to the other?

Together. Squarespace documents how to use Shopify with Squarespace: import Shopify products with a .csv file, link pages, buttons and navigation to your Shopify store, or transfer or point a Shopify domain. There is no built-in option for Shopify checkout buttons; the guide says they can be added with custom code, which Squarespace support can't help with.

From Squarespace to Shopify. Shopify's migration guide runs through 18 steps. You export products from the Squarespace Products panel, edit the CSV file to match Shopify's product template, then import it. Customers, reviews and order history are optional steps, and Shopify warns that some data can't be migrated by CSV.

From Shopify to Squarespace. Squarespace's guide says you can transfer domains and import products and contacts by downloading each as a .csv. It's not possible to import your previous site's layout, design, fonts or other content. Squarespace recommends keeping the old site online until the new one is complete.

How to choose between Shopify and Squarespace

Start from what your site mainly does.

  • Your site is mostly content, bookings or a portfolio, with a shop on the side: Squarespace's plans bundle the website, invoicing, memberships and selling. Check the 2% commerce transaction fee on Basic against the price of Core.
  • The store is the business: Shopify's plans are organized around the catalog, the checkout, sales channels and the App Store. Read our Shopify review for its strengths and limits.
  • You sell courses or memberships: compare Squarespace's digital products fee (7%, 5%, 1% or 0% by plan) with the cost of the app you would need on Shopify.
  • You depend on a specific tool: check that it exists as a Shopify app or as a Squarespace extension before anything else.
  • You already have a Squarespace site you like: Squarespace documents how to link it to a Shopify store, which lets you test a second setup without rebuilding the first.

If the alternative you are weighing is a different kind of platform, our comparisons of Shopify vs Wix and Shopify vs WooCommerce follow the same method.

In short

On October 7, 2026, Shopify Basic cost $29 a month paid yearly and Squarespace Basic $19 a month paid annually for US merchants, with the same published card rate of 2.9% plus 30 cents and a 2% commerce transaction fee on Squarespace Basic only. From Squarespace Core and Shopify Basic up, the fees on a standard card sale line up almost exactly, so the decision rests on the product: a website builder that sells, or a store platform with a large app marketplace. Price your own orders on both, list the tools you need, and check both pricing pages again before you sign up.

Frequently asked questions

Is Squarespace cheaper than Shopify?

The subscription is lower at the entry level. On October 7, 2026, Squarespace Basic cost $25 a month, or $19 paid annually, against $39 or $29 for Shopify Basic in the United States. Squarespace Basic adds a 2% commerce transaction fee on each sale, which Shopify does not charge when you use Shopify Payments. Squarespace Core, at $39 or $29, has no commerce transaction fee.

Does Squarespace charge transaction fees?

On its current plans, Squarespace charges a 2% commerce transaction fee on Basic and 0% on Core, Plus and Advanced, according to its Help Center on October 7, 2026. Digital products such as courses and memberships carry a separate fee: 7% on Basic, 5% on Core, 1% on Plus and 0% on Advanced. Payment processing rates come on top, starting at 2.9% + $0.30 for US cards with Squarespace Payments.

Are card rates the same on Shopify and Squarespace?

For standard US cards online, the published rates were close on October 7, 2026. Shopify Payments charges 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic, 2.7% + 30¢ on Grow and 2.5% + 30¢ on Advanced. Squarespace Payments charges 2.9% + $0.30 on Basic and Core, 2.7% + $0.30 on Plus and 2.5% + $0.30 on Advanced. International cards add 1% on Shopify and 1.5% on Squarespace.

Can I use Shopify and Squarespace together?

Squarespace's Help Center documents it. You can import your Shopify products into a Squarespace store with a .csv file, link from Squarespace pages to your Shopify store with buttons, images or navigation links, and transfer or point a Shopify domain. Squarespace has no built-in option for Shopify checkout buttons: its guide says they can be added with custom code, which its support team does not troubleshoot.

How do I move from Squarespace to Shopify?

Shopify's Help Center publishes a migration guide for Squarespace stores. You export your products from the Squarespace Products panel as a CSV file, edit the file to match Shopify's product CSV template, and import it. Customers, order history and reviews are optional steps. Shopify lists four methods: manual copy and paste, CSV import, third-party migration apps and hiring a Shopify Partner.
