50 WhatsApp Greeting Message Examples for Businesses (2026)

50 WhatsApp greeting message examples to copy for your business, by industry and language, plus how to set the automatic greeting in WhatsApp Business.

Nicolas ProvostMay 19, 2026 · 18 min read

Key takeaways

  • A WhatsApp Business greeting message is sent automatically when a customer writes for the first time or after 14 days of inactivity.
  • Keep it to two to four lines that give the brand name, the expected response time and one clear next step.
  • In the WhatsApp Business app you set it under Tools > Greeting message, and every contact receives the same text with no personalization.

A business greeting message, also called a welcome message, is the automated first reply a customer receives when they message your store, and on WhatsApp it is the first impression your Shopify brand makes inside the app your customers already use to talk to friends and family. Get it right and you turn a curious shopper into an engaged conversation. Get it wrong and you lose them. This guide gives you 50 ready-to-copy greeting examples sorted by industry, language, and use case, plus the exact setup steps for the free WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business API via Kanal.

These templates are written for Shopify stores running WhatsApp, on the WhatsApp Business app or through Kanal. Every example has been written to convert: short, warm, mobile-first, and ending with a clear next step. Copy them, swap the brackets for your own brand variables, and you are ready to ship.

1. What is a business greeting message on WhatsApp

A WhatsApp greeting message is an automated reply that fires the first time a contact messages your business, or after 14 days of inactivity (WhatsApp Help Center, read on October 7, 2026). It is the WhatsApp equivalent of a website chat widget's "Hi, how can we help?" but delivered inside the customer's most personal channel.

Greeting messages exist in three contexts:

  1. First contact: A shopper saves your number, scans a QR code, or clicks a click-to-chat link for the first time.
  2. Reactivation: An existing contact returns after weeks of silence. WhatsApp treats them like a new conversation.
  3. Trigger-based: On the WhatsApp Business API, you can send greetings on custom events like a new Shopify signup, the first order shipped, or after a QR code scan in-store.

The free WhatsApp Business app supports only the first two contexts. To unlock trigger-based greetings tied to Shopify events, you need the WhatsApp Business API through a Business Solution Provider like Kanal.

2. Anatomy of a high-converting greeting

Across the 50 examples below, you will notice every winning greeting message contains the same 5 elements:

  1. Identity: Brand name plus a tiny emoji or signature to confirm the contact reached the right business.
  2. Warmth: A human opener (Hi, Hey, Bonjour, Hola). Robots say "Welcome to XYZ Corp". Humans say "Hi there".
  3. Expectation: Response time. "We reply in under 5 minutes" or "Our team is online 9am to 7pm CET".
  4. Action: One clear next step. Browse the catalog, reply with a keyword, see the FAQ.
  5. Escape hatch: An alternative for shoppers who do not want to wait. A help center link, an AI chatbot, or a phone number.

Keep the message short, two to four lines. A chat preview only shows the start of a message, so front-load the brand name and the action.

9. How to set a greeting message in the WhatsApp Business app

For solo founders and stores under 10 orders a day, the free WhatsApp Business app is enough. Here is how to configure the greeting, following WhatsApp's Help Center (read on October 7, 2026):

  1. Open WhatsApp Business and go to the Tools tab.
  2. Tap Greeting message.
  3. Turn on Send greeting message.
  4. Tap the message to edit it, paste your greeting text, then confirm (OK on Android, Save on iPhone).
  5. Tap Recipients and choose: Everyone, everyone who is not in your address book, Everyone except…, or Only send to….
  6. Tap Save.

Two details from the same page are easy to miss. Your phone needs an active internet connection for the greeting to be sent, and greeting messages are switched off automatically when Meta Business Agent is connected to the account. If your greeting does not go out, also remember the 14-day rule: a customer who wrote to you in the last 14 days does not get it again.

Limitations of the free app:

  • Same greeting for everyone (no personalization with customer name).
  • No trigger on Shopify events.
  • Cannot send media (image, button, list).
  • Up to five devices per account, web or phone, or 10 with a Meta Verified subscription where available (Google Play listing).
  • Resets after 14 days of inactivity, not configurable.

Read the full WhatsApp Business Web and Desktop guide for the free app capabilities.

Greeting message, away message or quick reply?

The three sit side by side in the Tools tab and are easy to mix up. A greeting message goes out automatically on a first message, or after 14 days of inactivity. An away message goes out automatically when you are busy or outside the hours you set: see our away message templates. A quick reply is not automatic: it is a saved message that you insert yourself by typing a shortcut that starts with a slash (How to use quick replies). Any example in this guide can be saved as a quick reply if you prefer to send it by hand.

10. How to set up greetings with WhatsApp Business API (Kanal)

For stores doing more than 10 orders a day, the WhatsApp Business API via Kanal unlocks personalization, Shopify event triggers, and team inbox.

Steps with Kanal:

  1. Install the Kanal Shopify app and connect your WhatsApp Business number.
  2. Go to Automations then Greeting messages.
  3. Choose a trigger: first inbound message, new customer signup, first order shipped, abandoned checkout 30 min, or a custom Shopify event.
  4. Use merge tags: {{first_name}}, {{order_number}}, {{cart_total}}, {{store_name}}.
  5. Add media: product image, catalog link, quick reply buttons, or a list message.
  6. Test with the preview tool, then publish.

Example merge-tag greeting in Kanal:

Hi {{first_name}}! Welcome to {{store_name}}. We saw you added {{product_name}} to your cart. Reply YES to complete checkout with 10% off, or HELP if you have questions.

You can also pair greetings with a WhatsApp AI chatbot that qualifies the request 24/7 and escalates to a human only when needed.

11. Common greeting mistakes

Avoid these 4 traps:

  1. Walls of text: long blocks of text get skimmed or skipped on a phone screen. Cut, then cut again.
  2. No call to action: if the customer does not know what to do next, they bounce. Always end with one verb.
  3. Wrong language: detect the customer locale (via Kanal auto-detection) before sending. A Spanish customer reading a French greeting will leave.
  4. No fallback for after-hours: a greeting fires 24/7 but humans do not work 24/7. Pair it with an away message or an AI chatbot.

FAQ

What is a good WhatsApp greeting message? A good WhatsApp greeting message is short (under 200 characters), warm, and ends with a concrete next step. It identifies your brand, sets response expectations, and gives the customer one easy action: reply with a keyword, browse the catalog, or wait for a human agent.

How long should a WhatsApp greeting message be? Aim for 150 to 250 characters (2 to 4 lines). This is writing advice: WhatsApp's Help Center gives no character limit for greeting messages. A chat preview only shows the start of a message, so put the most important information (brand, action) in the first line. Avoid walls of text that look spammy.

Can I personalize WhatsApp greetings with the customer name? Yes, but only via the WhatsApp Business API. The free WhatsApp Business app sends the same greeting to every new contact. With Kanal, you can inject merge tags like {{first_name}}, {{order_number}}, or {{store_name}} for dynamic personalization at scale.

When should WhatsApp greetings trigger? Greetings should trigger on the first inbound message from a contact, and again after 14 days of inactivity (the default on the free app). On the API, you can also trigger greetings on Shopify events such as new customer signup or first order completion.

Do WhatsApp greetings work outside business hours? Yes. Greetings fire 24/7 regardless of business hours. To handle the conversation after the greeting, pair it with an away message or an AI chatbot that qualifies the request until a human agent is available the next morning.

How do I set a greeting message on WhatsApp Business? In the WhatsApp Business app, open the Tools tab and tap Greeting message. Turn on Send greeting message, tap the message to edit it, then choose the recipients: everyone, everyone not in your address book, everyone except some contacts, or only selected contacts. The greeting is then sent automatically to customers who message you for the first time, or after 14 days without activity in the chat.

What is the difference between a greeting message, an away message and a quick reply? All three are tools of the WhatsApp Business app. A greeting message is sent automatically when a customer writes for the first time or after 14 days of inactivity. An away message is sent automatically when you are busy or outside the hours you set. A quick reply is not automatic: it is a saved message that you insert yourself by typing a shortcut that starts with a slash.
