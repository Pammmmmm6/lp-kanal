Omnisend vs Klaviyo: Pricing and Features Compared

Omnisend vs Klaviyo for Shopify stores: prices at five list sizes, email allowances, who is billed, SMS rates, support, Shopify setup and migration.

Nicolas ProvostOct 7, 2026 · 11 min read

Key takeaways

  • Both tools price email on list size and have a free plan covering 250 contacts and 500 emails a month.
  • At 5,000 contacts on October 7, 2026, Omnisend Standard cost $81 a month, Omnisend Pro $90 and Klaviyo Email $100.
  • Omnisend Standard includes 12 emails per contact and Pro unlimited emails, while Klaviyo's Email plan includes 10 emails per active profile.
  • Klaviyo bills every profile that can be emailed, whereas Omnisend bills subscribers plus non-subscribers who received automated messages.

Omnisend and Klaviyo both price email marketing on the size of your list, and both have a free plan for 250 contacts and 500 emails a month. As of October 7, 2026, Omnisend's Standard plan started at $16 a month for 500 contacts and Klaviyo's Email plan at $20 a month for 500 active profiles; at 10,000 contacts, the pages showed $132 (Omnisend Standard), $150 (Omnisend Pro) and $150 (Klaviyo Email).

This comparison uses only what each company publishes: Omnisend's pricing page, Klaviyo's pricing page and Help Center, and both Shopify App Store listings, all read on October 7, 2026, in US dollars.

A worked example (fictional store)

A fictional Shopify store has 8,000 contacts and sends two campaigns a week to the whole list, plus automated flows that add about 2 emails per contact per month.

  • Volume: 8 campaigns × 8,000 contacts = 64,000 emails, plus 2 × 8,000 = 16,000 flow emails, so 80,000 emails a month.
  • Omnisend Standard: the 7,001 to 9,000 tier was $127 a month with 108,000 emails. 80,000 fits.
  • Omnisend Pro: emails are unlimited. We read $90 a month at 5,000 contacts and $150 at 10,000, and did not record the 7,001 to 9,000 tier, so read it on the page.
  • Klaviyo Email: the slider goes from 6,500 ($140) to 10,000 ($150), so 8,000 profiles are on the 10,000 step: $150 a month with 100,000 emails. 80,000 fits.

If the same store sent three campaigns a week, the volume would be 12 × 8,000 + 16,000 = 112,000 emails: above Omnisend Standard's 108,000 and above Klaviyo's 100,000 at this size. The next section explains what each tool does then.

Who do you pay for on each tool?

Omnisend. The list-size selector on its pricing page states: "You're billed for subscribers and non-subscribers who received automated messages. Unsubscribed contacts aren't billed."

Klaviyo. Its Help Center defines an active profile as any profile that can be emailed through Klaviyo, regardless of consent status, including people who shared an email address at checkout without opting in. Unsubscribed, bounced or suppressed profiles leave the billable count once the suppression is processed.

The two definitions agree on unsubscribes and differ on non-subscribers: Omnisend bills those who received automated messages, and Klaviyo bills every profile that can be emailed. A store with many checkout contacts who never opted in should count them carefully on both.

What happens when you pass a limit?

Omnisend. Its pricing FAQ says that if you exceed the email limit on the Free or Standard plan, you get the option to upgrade, and that you are always asked to confirm an account upgrade before it happens. Pro has unlimited monthly emails, with a fair use policy tied to its anti-spam policy.

Klaviyo. If your active profiles exceed your plan, you are moved to a plan that covers them at the next billing cycle. If you reach your email limit, your billing preference decides between automatic upgrade to the next tier, a one-time flexible overage, and paused sending until the cycle renews. According to its Help Center, automatic upgrade is the default on a new plan, a downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle, and mid-cycle downgrades are not refunded.

Omnisend's pricing FAQ adds that the Free plan gives access to the features of the paid plans, with two limits: 500 emails a month and 250 contacts.

How does each one connect to Shopify?

Omnisend's App Store listing describes campaigns and newsletters from ready-made templates, pop-ups and sign-up forms for list growth, and welcome, abandoned cart and post-purchase automation workflows. The listing shows it working with Shopify Checkout and Shopify Flow.

Klaviyo's integration guide describes bringing customer profile and order data into Klaviyo, adding people who accept email or SMS marketing at checkout to a list you choose, syncing Shopify Markets catalogs, enabling onsite tracking, and syncing profile data from Klaviyo back to Shopify. Its listing shows it working with Checkout, Customer accounts, Shopify Flow and the Shopify admin.

What does each vendor say about migration?

Omnisend publishes a page for migrating from Klaviyo. It says that, at no charge, its team recreates your workflows, segments, forms and templates after you grant access to your Klaviyo account during sign-up, and that it migrates all segments that can be matched in Omnisend. Its timeline: contact import typically completes within 1 to 24 hours, and a full migration, including rebuilding automations, verifying your sender domain and testing, usually takes 5 business days. It advises keeping your old platform active until automations are confirmed and reports are exported.

Klaviyo's integration guide gives one instruction for stores arriving from another email service: disconnect the previous tool from Shopify before integrating Klaviyo, or double opt-in emails could be sent to your existing subscribers.

Whichever direction you go, plan one overlap billing cycle, and warm up the new sending domain on your most engaged segment first.

How to choose between Omnisend and Klaviyo

  • Small list, tight budget. Read the first three rows of the price table. The free plans have the same limits; support differs, as each vendor states it.
  • You email often. Compare allowances: 12 times your list on Omnisend Standard, unlimited on Omnisend Pro, 10 per profile on Klaviyo's Email plan. Run the arithmetic from the worked example with your own cadence.
  • SMS is central. On Omnisend, SMS credits attach to the Pro plan. On Klaviyo, mobile messages attach to any plan. Compare the monthly totals at your volume, with carrier fees clarified.
  • You want WhatsApp or mobile push in the same tool. Klaviyo's listing names both. Omnisend's names web push.
  • You are still shortlisting. Klaviyo vs Mailchimp applies the same method to another tool, and our Klaviyo alternatives page and ranking of the best Shopify email marketing apps widen the list. For text messaging alone, see the best Shopify SMS apps.

Where does WhatsApp fit next to your email tool?

WhatsApp can run in a dedicated app next to either tool. Kanal, our WhatsApp marketing app for Shopify, integrates with Klaviyo: Klaviyo segments sync to Kanal and WhatsApp messages are triggered from Klaviyo flows, while email and SMS stay in Klaviyo. Kanal has no integration with Omnisend. Next to Omnisend, Kanal works from your Shopify data for abandoned cart recovery and campaigns on WhatsApp, and your email and SMS program stays where it is.

Omnisend vs Klaviyo in short

Both tools bill by list size with matching free plans. Omnisend publishes two paid plans, Standard with 12 emails per contact and Pro with unlimited emails and SMS credits as an add-on. Klaviyo publishes an Email plan with 10 emails per active profile, mobile messages on any plan and a wider set of add-on products. Read both pages at your list size and your sending cadence, clarify what each SMS rate includes, and keep the old tool running for one cycle if you switch.

Frequently asked questions

Is Omnisend cheaper than Klaviyo?

At the list sizes we read on October 7, 2026, Omnisend's Standard plan was priced below Klaviyo's Email plan: $81 against $100 a month at 5,000 contacts, and $132 against $150 at 10,000. Omnisend's Pro plan, which has unlimited emails, was $90 and $150 at the same sizes. The two tools do not bill the same contacts, so price your own list on both pages.

Do Omnisend and Klaviyo have free plans?

Yes. As of October 7, 2026, both free plans cover 250 contacts and 500 emails a month. Omnisend adds 500 web push notifications and says its 24/7 live chat and email support covers every plan, the free one included. Klaviyo adds $5 of mobile messages a month and email support for the first 60 days.

How many emails can I send on each plan?

Klaviyo's Email plan included 10 emails per active profile per month at every slider step we read, for example 50,000 emails at 5,000 profiles. Omnisend's Standard plan includes 12 times your list, for example 60,000 emails at 5,000 contacts, and its Pro plan lists unlimited emails under a fair use policy.

How does SMS pricing compare between Omnisend and Klaviyo?

On October 7, 2026, Omnisend's calculator estimated $50 a month for 5,882 SMS sent to the United States, a volume rate of $0.0085 per SMS, as an add-on to its Pro plan. Klaviyo's calculator showed $70 a month for 5,000 SMS to the United States from a toll-free number, provider fees included. Check what each rate includes before comparing them.

Does Omnisend help with migration from Klaviyo?

Omnisend publishes a migration page for Klaviyo users. It says its team recreates your workflows, segments, forms and templates at no charge once you grant access to your Klaviyo account, that contact import typically completes within 1 to 24 hours, and that a full migration usually takes 5 business days. It advises keeping the old platform active until automations are confirmed.
