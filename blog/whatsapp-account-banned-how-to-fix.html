WhatsApp Account Banned? How to Unban Your Number (2026)

My WhatsApp number is banned: how to get unbanned step by step, temporary vs permanent bans, and how businesses stay ban-proof on the official API.

Nicolas ProvostMay 20, 2026 · 12 min read

Key takeaways

  • A ban screen with a countdown is temporary and lifts on its own, usually within a few hours to a few days.
  • With no timer, the ban is permanent and the only route is the in-app Request a review, which WhatsApp says mostly completes within 24 hours.
  • Reinstalling the app, switching devices or installing a modified app like GB WhatsApp while you wait can make the ban worse.

Personal account banned or blocked? Kanal is a WhatsApp app for Shopify stores: we cannot review, unban or contact WhatsApp about your account. Use the appeal inside the app (Settings, Help, Contact us) or the WhatsApp support form. This guide explains what to do, step by step.

Quick answer

If you see a countdown, the ban is temporary and the account unlocks on its own, usually within a few hours to a few days. If you see "This account is not allowed to use WhatsApp" or "This account can no longer use WhatsApp" with no timer, the ban is permanent and your only route is the in-app review request: WhatsApp says most reviews are completed within 24 hours (WhatsApp Help Center). Do not reinstall, do not switch devices, and do not use a modified app, as each of those extends the penalty instead of lifting it.

Introduction: When WhatsApp Locks You Out

Few things are as alarming as opening WhatsApp and seeing a message that your account has been banned, or that your phone number is "not authorized to use WhatsApp". For an individual it means losing chats and contacts. For a business that runs customer support and marketing on WhatsApp, a ban can freeze an entire sales channel overnight.

The good news is that a ban can be explained, a temporary one expires on its own, and the triggers are avoidable once you understand them. WhatsApp does not ban accounts randomly. Its systems look for specific patterns, and the patterns that get businesses in trouble are very different from the ones that affect personal users.

This guide explains the difference between a temporary and a permanent ban, walks through the most common reasons accounts get flagged, shows you exactly how to appeal, and most importantly covers how to message customers at scale without ever putting your number at risk.

Temporary Ban vs Permanent Ban: Know the Difference

WhatsApp uses two distinct enforcement levels, and telling them apart is the first thing to do.

A temporary ban is a warning with a clock. You will see a screen that says your account is temporarily banned, usually with a countdown showing how long you must wait. Durations range from a few hours to a few days. Temporary bans escalate: the first one might last hours, the next ones longer, and repeated bans can end with the account converted to a permanent ban. While the timer runs, there is nothing to do but wait, and crucially, stop the behavior that caused it.

A permanent ban removes your account entirely. The message simply says "This account is not allowed to use WhatsApp" with no countdown, only an option to request a review. The "your phone number is not authorized to use WhatsApp" error is the same situation. A permanent ban means WhatsApp's systems concluded that the account broke the Terms of Service in a serious or repeated way.

If you see a countdown, you have a temporary ban and time to correct course. If you see a review request and no timer, you have a permanent ban and your only path is an appeal.

"This Account Can No Longer Use WhatsApp": What Each Message Means

WhatsApp shows a handful of different screens, and the exact wording tells you which situation you are in. Match what is on your screen to the list below. If you see none of them and only one contact stops receiving your messages, the question is a different one: how to know if someone blocked you on WhatsApp.

"This account can no longer use WhatsApp" or "This account is not allowed to use WhatsApp". This is a permanent ban. There is no countdown, only a button to request a review. The account stays locked until Meta's team overturns the decision.

"Your phone number is not authorized to use WhatsApp" or "not authorised". Same thing as above, worded differently depending on your app version and region. Treat it as a permanent ban and appeal.

"You have been temporarily banned" with a timer. A warning with a clock. The account unlocks itself when the countdown ends. Nothing you do will speed it up, but plenty of things will make it worse.

A screen saying WhatsApp is reviewing the account. This is not a ban: no decision has been made yet, and WhatsApp is checking the account, either because you appealed or because its systems spotted unusual activity. That status has its own guide: how long a WhatsApp account review takes and what to do meanwhile.

"Register your number to use WhatsApp" on a working number. Not a ban at all, usually a session or SIM issue. Re-verify the number before assuming the worst.

How to Get Unbanned From WhatsApp Quickly

There is no trick that removes a ban instantly, and every service that claims otherwise is selling you nothing. What does exist is a fastest realistic path, and a list of moves that make things worse.

The fastest path. Submit one review request from the banned device, with a short factual explanation, and then leave it alone. WhatsApp reviews one phone number per appeal, and states that contacting it outside the review process does not expedite the review or change the decision. Its ban message says most reviews are completed within 24 hours; some take longer. If the screen shows no option to request a review, WhatsApp says the ban is final and cannot be appealed.

What to write. State plainly that you believe the ban is a mistake, describe in one sentence how the number is used, and confirm you are not using a modified app or a bulk-messaging tool. If you have stopped the behavior that triggered the ban, say so. Long emotional messages do not help, the review is largely procedural.

What makes it worse. Reinstalling WhatsApp, moving the SIM to another phone, creating a second account on the same number, or installing GB WhatsApp or any other mod while you wait. Each of these looks like evasion to the detection system and can turn a temporary ban into a permanent one.

If the appeal is refused. The decision is generally final for that number. A business in that position needs a fresh number connected to the official WhatsApp Business API, which is the only sanctioned way to message customers at volume without risking the same outcome again.

The Most Common Reasons WhatsApp Bans Accounts

Bans almost always trace back to a short list of behaviors. Here are the ones that matter for businesses.

Bulk messaging and spam. Sending the same or similar message to a large number of people in a short window is a classic trigger. WhatsApp's spam detection is sensitive to volume and repetition, and the regular Business app was never designed for mass sends.

Messaging people who have not saved your number. This cause is easy to underestimate. When you message contacts who do not have you saved, a higher share of them block or report you. A burst of outreach to a cold list is a fast track to a ban.

Too many blocks and reports. Every time a recipient blocks you or taps "Report", it counts against your account. A cluster of blocks in a short period, even from a modest number of messages, signals unwanted contact and can trigger enforcement on its own.

Unofficial modified apps. GB WhatsApp, YoWhatsApp and similar mods are unauthorized clients. WhatsApp detects them and bans the accounts that use them, frequently on a permanent basis with no warning. Do not confuse them with WhatsApp Plus, which is now the name of an optional subscription offered by Meta (WhatsApp Help Center).

Automation and unapproved third-party tools. Bots, scripts, or grey-market tools that automate the consumer app violate the Terms of Service. The official WhatsApp Business API is the only sanctioned way to automate. Unofficial scheduling apps fall in the same category: our guide on how to schedule a WhatsApp message sticks to the options WhatsApp allows.

Broad Terms of Service violations. Sharing illegal content, impersonation, harassment, harvesting contacts, or creating accounts purely to spam will all get an account removed.

For a business, the first three reasons are the real danger, and a store that runs marketing from the regular app can trip all three at once.

How to Appeal a WhatsApp Ban Step by Step

If you believe your account was banned by mistake, or you have corrected the behavior and want to contest a temporary ban, you can request a review. Follow these steps.

  1. Open WhatsApp on the banned device. Even when banned, the app shows the ban screen with an appeal option, so do not delete it.
  2. Tap "Request a review" or "Support". On a permanent ban this button sits directly under the ban message. On some versions you confirm your number with a one-time code first.
  3. Verify your phone number if prompted, by entering the 6-digit SMS code so WhatsApp can tie the appeal to your number.
  4. Write a clear, honest explanation. State that you believe the ban was an error, briefly describe how you use the account, and confirm you are not using a modified app or bulk-messaging tool. Keep it short and factual. Do not send multiple requests, as that slows the queue.
  5. Submit and wait. Most reviews are completed within 24 hours, according to the WhatsApp Help Center, and you receive a WhatsApp notification once the decision is made.
  6. If the review succeeds, your account is reinstated with chats intact on that device. If it is upheld, the decision is generally final for that number.

You can also email WhatsApp support from the app via Settings, Help, Contact us, which can help if the in-app appeal does not load.

How to Recover Access After a Ban

Recovery depends on which ban you are dealing with.

For a temporary ban, recovery is automatic. When the countdown reaches zero the account unlocks itself. Do not try to bypass it by reinstalling, switching devices, or using a mod, as that escalates the penalty. Use the waiting time to clean up: delete any bulk-messaging tool, remove unsaved contacts you mass-messaged, and rethink how you reach people.

For a permanent ban, the review request above is your only built-in route. If the appeal is denied, the number can no longer be used with WhatsApp. A business in that position generally has to move to a fresh number, and this time onto the official WhatsApp Business API so the channel is compliant from day one.

Whatever you do, never buy a "ban removal" service. They cannot override Meta's systems, and handing your number to a third party only adds risk.

Why Business Bulk Messaging Gets Flagged

A common path to a business ban is an honest mistake: treating the WhatsApp Business app like a marketing platform. It is not one.

The WhatsApp Business app is a polished version of the consumer app. It is built for a small team replying to customers one conversation at a time. It has no real concept of consent tracking, no pre-approved message templates, no sending infrastructure, and no rate management. When a store loads a few hundred customer numbers and starts broadcasting a promotion, WhatsApp sees exactly the footprint of a spammer: high volume, repeated content, many recipients who never saved the sender.

Broadcast lists do not solve this either. They cap at 256 contacts and, more importantly, a broadcast only reaches people who have saved your number. So the recipients who have not saved you, precisely the ones most likely to block or report, are also the ones who never receive it.

The result is predictable. The first campaign might go out fine. The second triggers a temporary ban. A third attempt after the timer expires can push the account permanently offline, taking your live customer conversations with it.

How the Official WhatsApp Business API Keeps You Compliant

The compliant way to message customers at scale is the WhatsApp Business API, the official channel Meta built for exactly this purpose. Instead of a phone app, it is a server-side connection with rules baked in to keep senders legitimate.

The API enforces compliance in several ways. Marketing and notification messages must use templates that Meta reviews and approves before they can be sent, which keeps content within policy. The API is designed around explicit opt-in, so you message people who agreed to hear from you. It also includes a quality rating that gives gentle, visible feedback long before any ban, so problems are caught early. Used properly, an API number can send to large opt-in audiences without the spam flags that sink the consumer app.

The catch is that the API is infrastructure, not an app. You connect to it through a Business Solution Provider that supplies the inbox, the automation, and the template management. For a Shopify store, that provider can be an app such as Kanal.

Conclusion: Stay on the Right Side of WhatsApp

A WhatsApp ban feels sudden, but it rarely is. Temporary bans are warnings with a timer, permanent bans follow serious or repeated violations, and the "not authorized" error is simply the permanent ban under another name. If you are hit by one, identify which type it is, request a review honestly, and never try to bypass enforcement with mods or shady services.

For businesses the lesson is sharper. Bulk messaging from the WhatsApp Business app is a fast route to a ban, because to WhatsApp it is indistinguishable from spam. The official WhatsApp Business API exists precisely so that stores can message customers at scale without that risk, with reviewed templates, real opt-in, and a quality score that warns you early.

If you run a Shopify store and want to use WhatsApp for marketing and support without gambling your account, move onto the API from the start.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a temporary WhatsApp ban last?

A temporary WhatsApp ban usually shows a countdown timer and lasts anywhere from a few hours to a few days. Repeat offenses extend the window and can end in a permanent ban. The exact duration is decided by WhatsApp's automated systems and is not negotiable while the timer is running.

Can a permanently banned WhatsApp account be recovered?

Sometimes. If you believe the ban was a mistake, open WhatsApp, tap "Request a review", and submit the appeal. Meta's team reviews it manually and can reinstate the account if no real violation occurred. WhatsApp says most reviews are completed within 24 hours. If the ban is upheld, the number cannot be reused with WhatsApp and you would need a different phone number to start fresh.

Why does WhatsApp say 'your phone number is not authorized'?

That message means WhatsApp has blocked the number from using the service, almost always because of a Terms of Service violation such as bulk or automated messaging, an unofficial modified app, or a high volume of blocks and spam reports from recipients. It is the same as a ban. Submit a review request from the app to contest it.

Can sending bulk messages get my WhatsApp banned?

Yes. Sending the same message to many people, especially contacts who have not saved your number, is exactly the pattern WhatsApp's spam detection looks for. The regular WhatsApp Business app is built for one-to-one conversations, not mass outreach. To message customers at scale safely, you need the official WhatsApp Business API with opt-in contacts and pre-approved templates.

Is using GB WhatsApp or WhatsApp Plus safe?

GB WhatsApp, YoWhatsApp and similar mods are unofficial apps that violate WhatsApp's Terms of Service. WhatsApp actively detects them and bans the accounts that use them, often permanently. They also lack proper encryption guarantees and can expose your data. WhatsApp Plus is a different case today: it is the name of an optional subscription offered by Meta, according to the WhatsApp Help Center. Always use the official WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app.

How do you get unbanned from WhatsApp quickly?

For a temporary ban, the fastest route is waiting: the account unlocks automatically when the countdown ends, and trying to bypass it extends the penalty. For a permanent ban, open WhatsApp, tap Request a review, and send one short, honest appeal. WhatsApp says most reviews are completed within 24 hours, and paid "unban services" cannot speed anything up.

Why is my WhatsApp account banned?

Bans almost always trace back to Terms of Service violations: sending bulk or repetitive messages, messaging many people who have not saved your number, accumulating blocks and spam reports, using a modified app like GB WhatsApp, or running unauthorized automation. WhatsApp does not ban at random, so identifying which behavior triggered it is the first step toward fixing it.

What does 'This account can no longer use WhatsApp' mean?

It is the wording WhatsApp uses for a permanent ban. There is no countdown on that screen, only a button to request a review. The account stays locked until Meta's team overturns the decision. The same situation is sometimes worded as "This account is not allowed to use WhatsApp" or "your phone number is not authorized to use WhatsApp" depending on your app version and region.
