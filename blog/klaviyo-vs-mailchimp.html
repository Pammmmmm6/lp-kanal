Klaviyo vs Mailchimp: Pricing and Features Compared

Klaviyo vs Mailchimp for Shopify stores: prices at five list sizes, how each counts contacts, sending limits, overages, SMS and the Shopify integration.

Nicolas ProvostOct 7, 2026 · 12 min read

Key takeaways

  • At 5,000 contacts, Klaviyo's Email plan costs $100 a month, against $75 for Mailchimp Essentials and $100 for Mailchimp Standard.
  • Klaviyo stops billing suppressed profiles, while Mailchimp counts unsubscribed contacts until you archive them, so the same list can land in different tiers.
  • Both free plans cover 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, but Klaviyo's includes flows and Mailchimp's does not.

Klaviyo and Mailchimp are both priced on the size of your list, with a free plan for up to 250 contacts: Klaviyo's Email plan starts at $20 a month for 500 active profiles, and Mailchimp's paid plans start at $13 a month (Essentials) and $20 a month (Standard) for 500 contacts, as of October 7, 2026. The prices stay close as the list grows, so the decision usually rests on how each tool counts contacts, how many emails the plan lets you send and how deep you want the Shopify data to go.

This comparison uses only what each company publishes: Klaviyo's pricing page and Help Center, Mailchimp's pricing page and Help Center, and both apps' Shopify App Store listings. Everything was read on October 7, 2026, with prices in US dollars.

A worked example (fictional store)

Take a fictional Shopify store with 4,200 people it can email and 1,800 past unsubscribes still stored in the account.

  • On Klaviyo: unsubscribed profiles are not billable once processed, so the store pays for 4,200 active profiles. The slider goes from 3,500 to 5,000, so the store is on the 5,000 step: $100 a month.
  • On Mailchimp, as is: unsubscribed contacts count, so the total is 4,200 + 1,800 = 6,000 contacts, in the 5,001 to 10,000 tier: $110 a month on Essentials or $135 on Standard.
  • On Mailchimp, after archiving the 1,800 unsubscribes: the total is 6,000 - 1,800 = 4,200 contacts, in the 2,501 to 5,000 tier: $75 a month on Essentials or $100 on Standard.

The point of the arithmetic: the same store is quoted three different totals depending on the counting rule and on list hygiene. Check your own total against the tier boundaries before and after cleaning.

How does each tool count the contacts you pay for?

This is where the two vendors' documentation differs the most.

Klaviyo. Its Help Center defines an active profile as any profile that can be emailed through Klaviyo, regardless of consent status. That includes people who shared an email address at checkout without opting in. Profiles marked as unsubscribed, bounced or manually suppressed stay in the account for reporting but are not in the billable count once the suppression is processed, which can take 72 hours to show.

Mailchimp. Its Help Center states that subscribed, unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts are included in your contact count, and that archived, cleaned and deleted contacts don't count toward your contact total or the price of your plan. The count is the number of contacts stored across all of your audiences.

In practice:

  • On Klaviyo, the routine is suppression: profiles you no longer market to leave the bill.
  • On Mailchimp, the routine is archiving: unsubscribed contacts keep counting until you archive them.
  • On both, a shopper who reached checkout without subscribing can count.

What happens when you go over a limit?

On Mailchimp's paid plans, sending continues and the bill grows. According to its article on additional charges, if your contact total or send count exceeds your tier, service is not interrupted: you are charged for an add-on contact block, or several, on your monthly bill. The price and size of a block depend on your plan and tier and are shown in your account. On Standard, a block of 1,000 additional contacts includes 12,000 additional sends.

On Mailchimp's Free plan, sending stops. Above 250 contacts, a hold is placed on live and test emails until you upgrade or reduce your contact total.

On Klaviyo, the list and the volume are handled separately. If your active profiles exceed your plan, you are moved to a plan that covers them at the next billing cycle. If you reach your sending limit, your billing preference decides: automatic upgrade to the next tier, a one-time flexible overage, or no sending until the cycle renews. On the free plan, the account is not upgraded but cannot send above its limits.

Downgrades. Klaviyo schedules a downgrade for the next billing cycle and does not refund mid-cycle downgrades. Mailchimp's pricing page says downgrades go into effect at the end of the monthly billing period, without a refund for charges already billed, and that you keep access to the features until the period ends.

SMS

Klaviyo adds mobile messages to any plan and bills a per-message rate that depends on the channel and the country. Its calculator showed $15 a month for 1,000 SMS sent to the United States from a toll-free number, $70 for 5,000 and $125 for 10,000, provider fees included. Mailchimp's plan table lists "SMS add-on" for Essentials, Standard and Premium and "Not included" for Free. The plan table we read did not display a per-message rate, so check the rate in your account before you compare.

How does each one connect to Shopify?

Klaviyo documents its integration in a Help Center guide. It brings customer profile and order data into Klaviyo, can add people who accept email or SMS marketing at checkout to a list you choose, syncs Shopify Markets catalogs, enables onsite tracking for browsing events, and can sync profile data from Klaviyo back to Shopify. Its listing says new stores get a guided setup (sign-up form, welcome email and abandoned checkout flow) inside the Shopify admin.

Mailchimp describes its integration on its App Store listing: welcome, abandoned cart and back-in-stock automations, Shopify segments synced to personalize email and SMS campaigns, single-use discount codes, Shopify customer events turned into segments, and attributed revenue tracked in Shopify.

Both listings show the app as working with Shopify Checkout and Shopify Flow.

What should you know before switching?

Klaviyo's integration guide carries one explicit warning: if you are migrating from another email service that is still connected to your Shopify store, disconnect it before you integrate Klaviyo. Otherwise, double opt-in emails could be sent to your existing subscribers.

Three checks that apply in both directions:

  1. Count your list the way the new tool will. Unsubscribed contacts count on Mailchimp until archived. Checkout contacts without consent can count on both.
  2. Price your sending, not only your list. A weekly newsletter plus flows can pass 10 emails per contact in a busy month.
  3. Run both tools for a full billing cycle on a small segment before you cancel the old one, and export the reports you want to keep.

How to choose between Klaviyo and Mailchimp

Start from your situation, then read the matching row of the tables above.

  • List under 250 contacts. Both free plans cover you with 500 emails a month. Klaviyo's includes flows and Mailchimp's does not include automation flows, according to each plan table.
  • Many unsubscribed or inactive contacts. The two counting rules give different totals for the same list. Do the arithmetic from the worked example with your own numbers.
  • Several people on the account. Mailchimp limits seats by plan (3 on Essentials, 5 on Standard). Klaviyo's pricing page does not list a seat limit.
  • SMS is part of the plan. Klaviyo publishes its per-message cost on the pricing page. Ask Mailchimp for its add-on rate at your volume.
  • You want other options first. Our Klaviyo alternatives comparison and our ranking of the best Shopify email marketing apps cover more tools, and Omnisend vs Klaviyo applies the same method to a third one.

Where does WhatsApp fit next to your email tool?

Email and SMS stay in the tool you choose here. WhatsApp can run in a dedicated app next to it. Kanal, our WhatsApp marketing app for Shopify, integrates with Klaviyo: your Klaviyo segments sync to Kanal and WhatsApp messages are triggered from Klaviyo flows, while email and SMS stay in Klaviyo. Kanal has no integration with Mailchimp: with Mailchimp, WhatsApp campaigns run from your Shopify data in Kanal, alongside your email program.

Klaviyo vs Mailchimp in short

Both tools charge by list size and both start with a 250-contact free plan. Klaviyo bills active profiles and includes 10 emails per profile. Mailchimp bills stored contacts, unsubscribes included, with 10, 12 or 15 sends per contact depending on the plan, and limits seats, audiences and automation by plan. Read each pricing page at your own list size, count your list both ways, and add text messages at your real volume before you compare totals.

Frequently asked questions

Is Klaviyo more expensive than Mailchimp?

It depends on the plan and on who is in your list. On October 7, 2026, Klaviyo's Email plan was $100 a month for 5,000 active profiles, and Mailchimp showed $75 for Essentials and $100 for Standard at 2,501 to 5,000 contacts. Mailchimp counts unsubscribed contacts in the total, and Klaviyo stops billing suppressed profiles, so the same list can land in different tiers.

How do Klaviyo and Mailchimp count contacts?

Klaviyo bills active profiles: any profile that can be emailed, regardless of consent status. Unsubscribed and suppressed profiles leave the billable count once processed. Mailchimp's Help Center says subscribed, unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts are included in your contact count, and that archived, cleaned and deleted contacts are not. Archiving is the lever on Mailchimp, suppression on Klaviyo.

Do Klaviyo and Mailchimp have a free plan?

Both do. As of October 7, 2026, Klaviyo's free plan covers 250 active profiles and 500 emails a month, with $5 of mobile messages and email support for the first 60 days. Mailchimp's Free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with a daily limit of 250, one seat, one audience and email support for the first 30 days.

What happens if I go over my limit on each tool?

On Mailchimp's paid plans, sending continues and you are charged for add-on contact blocks on your monthly bill. On its Free plan, sending is put on hold. On Klaviyo, exceeding your active profiles moves you to a higher tier at the next billing cycle, and reaching a sending limit follows your billing preference: automatic upgrade, a one-time flexible overage, or paused sending.

Can I send SMS with Klaviyo and Mailchimp?

Yes, with different packaging. Klaviyo adds mobile messages to any plan at a per-message rate: its calculator showed $15 a month for 1,000 SMS sent to the United States from a toll-free number on October 7, 2026. Mailchimp lists SMS as an add-on to its paid plans and as not included in the Free plan. Its plan table does not show a rate.
