Affiliate program FAQ

How does the Kanal affiliate program work?

Sign up for the affiliate program, get your unique referral link and share it with your audience. You earn a 15% recurring commission for every paying customer you refer.

How much can I earn?

You earn 15% of every payment made by the customers you refer, for as long as they remain paying customers. On Scale at 149€ per month, each referral pays you 22.35€ every month.

When and how do I get paid?

Commissions are paid monthly by bank transfer or PayPal. Each commission has a 30-day pending period to account for refunds.

Who can join the affiliate program?

Anyone can join. Content creators, agency owners, e-commerce consultants or anyone who loves Kanal: every affiliate is welcome.

Is there a minimum payout?

Yes, the minimum payout is 50€. Once your available balance reaches 50€, it is included in the next monthly payout.
