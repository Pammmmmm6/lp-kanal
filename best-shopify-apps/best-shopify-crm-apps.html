Does Shopify have a built-in CRM?

Is Shopify a CRM? No. Shopify describes itself as a commerce platform that helps you sell online and in person. Its admin does cover the basics of customer management. According to Shopify's help center, every new customer who places an order is added to your customer list. Each customer profile holds orders and draft orders, contact details, notes, tags, email, SMS and WhatsApp marketing preferences, and timeline events. Customer segments are dynamic, rule-based lists: you combine filters such as amount spent, number of orders, location or subscription status, and customers enter or leave the segment automatically.

That is a solid customer database. Shopify's own ecommerce CRM guide says this native profile and segmentation system can cover the basics for businesses that don't need a dedicated CRM's deeper contact and lead management tools. A CRM app adds what happens around the data: the conversation next to the order, a team that shares the inbox, a pipeline for larger deals, and follow-ups triggered by customer events. Shopify's own CRM integration guide points to the App Store for that layer. It names HubSpot, Salesforce sync apps, Zoho CRM and Endear, and calls Klaviyo Shopify's CRM partner for over a decade. Kanal adds a WhatsApp layer on the same Shopify data, so the customer profile, the conversation and the automation sit in one place.

What to look for in a Shopify CRM app

A CRM only pays off if your team opens it every day. A few points decide whether it will.

  • Where your team works. Pick the app that sits where customer conversations already happen: a WhatsApp inbox, a helpdesk, an email platform or a sales CRM.
  • Shopify data sync. Check which records sync (customers, orders, products, abandoned checkouts), how fast and in which direction. HubSpot's knowledge base, for example, says records sync within 10 minutes of a change and orders sync one way from Shopify.
  • The customer profile. Order history, total spend, tags and past conversations on one screen, so nobody asks a customer to repeat themselves.
  • Segments you can act on. Shopify segments already filter by amount spent, orders or location. A CRM app should turn a segment into a campaign or a flow.
  • Channels and team features. WhatsApp, live chat and social inboxes bring the conversation into the profile. Look for assignment, internal notes, quick replies and, for high-ticket or B2B sales, pipelines with stages.
  • Automation from Shopify events. A new order, a high-value purchase or an inactive customer should trigger the next message without anyone exporting a list.
  • How the price grows. Plans scale with contacts (Klaviyo), tickets (Gorgias), users (Kustomer), synced orders (Salesforce Sync) or AI messages (Wizybot). Price your next twelve months, not your first, and weigh it against what a customer is worth over time (see our customer lifetime value guide).

#1 Kanal WhatsApp, a WhatsApp CRM built on your Shopify data

Kanal is a WhatsApp marketing and sales app for Shopify stores, used here as a WhatsApp CRM. It syncs your Shopify customers, orders and tags, so each contact profile shows order history, total spend, tags and past conversations. Several agents share one WhatsApp Business number in the team inbox: they assign conversations, add internal notes and use quick replies, with the Shopify order next to the chat. Segments group customers by purchase history, order value, tags and location, and Klaviyo segments sync in too. Follow-ups start from customer events such as a first order, a VIP purchase or 60 days of inactivity. Kanal sends WhatsApp and SMS messages (see its SMS marketing software for Shopify page), so email stays in your other tools, and it integrates with Klaviyo, Gorgias, Zendesk, Crisp, Recharge, Loyoly and Splio. It runs on the official WhatsApp Business API and is rated 5.0 from 125 reviews on the Shopify App Store (October 7, 2026). Pro costs $89/month with 1 user, the inbox, cart recovery and campaigns. Scale costs $149/month with 5 users, segments and the AI agent, which answers customers in more than 96 languages and hands over to your team. Segments and the AI agent are not part of Pro, and WhatsApp fees are passed through at cost. See the WhatsApp CRM page.

#2 Klaviyo, customer profiles and AI segments for email, SMS and WhatsApp

Klaviyo is a marketing platform, and Shopify's CRM integration guide calls it Shopify's CRM partner for over a decade. Its listing says it unifies and activates real-time data from Shopify and 350+ apps. You build AI-powered segments and flow triggers from historical and real-time data, then reach customers by email, SMS, WhatsApp, mobile push and pop-ups, with automated workflows from Klaviyo's AI agents. Customer Hub and Customer Agent aim to turn service moments into shopping moments, and the email plan adds predictive analytics. Email is free up to 250 contacts and SMS up to 150 credits. Paid plans start at $15/month for SMS (up to 1,250 credits) and $20/month for email (251 to 500 contacts), and grow with your list (our Klaviyo pricing guide details the tiers). Rated 4.7/5 from 3,358 reviews, Klaviyo is worth a look if email and SMS drive your marketing and you want segments built on live Shopify data. Kanal's Klaviyo integration, on the Scale plan, sends Klaviyo segments to WhatsApp campaigns.

Its listing describes one unified inbox for email, chat, social, voice and SMS, an AI Agent that resolves support end to end, an AI Shopping Assistant that recommends products, 100+ integrations and analytics that turn conversations into insights. The CRM part is the customer timeline: Gorgias's help center explains that it shows ticket history and Shopify order data, with each order's number, status, date, total and item count, so agents answer with context. Tagging, chat assignment and multi-store support are in the feature list, while customer segmentation is not. Starter costs $10/month (3 agents, 50 tickets, $0.40 per extra ticket), Basic $60/month (300 tickets, unlimited agents), Pro $360/month (2,000 tickets) and Advanced $900/month (5,000 tickets), each with a 7-day free trial (see our Gorgias pricing guide). Goal-aware automations run on Active Intelligence, and the platform connects to 950+ apps. Segmentation, tagging, site tracking and order status triggers appear in its features. The Shopify integration is available from Starter at $19/month ($180/year), which includes the CRM, site tracking, abandoned cart and SMS. Plus costs $59/month, Pro $89/month (with the Shopify Plus integration) and Enterprise $159/month (custom objects, 5 users included), each with a 14-day free trial. Rated 4.5/5 from 260 reviews, ActiveCampaign is worth a look if you want email automation and a CRM in one subscription.

It includes an online store editor and themes to build your storefront, plus customer management in the admin: customer profiles with orders, notes and tags, and rule-based customer segments. If you need a full CRM, you connect one from the Shopify App Store, from HubSpot or a Salesforce sync app to a WhatsApp CRM like Kanal.

Which CRM is best to integrate with Shopify?

It depends on where your team talks to customers. If it happens on WhatsApp, Kanal keeps each conversation next to the Shopify order and triggers follow-ups from Shopify events. If your sales team uses HubSpot, its official app syncs Shopify customers, products, orders and abandoned checkouts. Salesforce and Zoho CRM connect through third-party sync apps such as Salesforce Sync by CRM Perks or Skylio.
