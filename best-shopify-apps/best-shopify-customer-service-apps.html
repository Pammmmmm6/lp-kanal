How we compared the 12 apps

We installed each app on a test Shopify store, connected email and website chat (plus WhatsApp and Instagram where supported), and ran the same twenty tickets through it: order tracking, a return, an address change, a pre-sale sizing question and an angry complaint, on desktop and mobile. We scored six dimensions: Shopify data depth (order actions inside the ticket), AI automation quality and how cleanly it escalates, channel coverage in one inbox, self-service tools (help center, FAQ bot, order portal), reporting on response and resolution times, and pricing model at three and ten agents. Pricing was read from the Shopify App Store in September 2026, and the listings of Gorgias, Kanal, Zendesk, Re:amaze and Tidio were read again on October 7, 2026. We excluded white-label resellers and tools without a meaningful update in the last twelve months.

Why customer service makes or breaks a Shopify store

Support quality has a direct, measurable effect on revenue. Pre-sale questions answered quickly convert into orders, and post-sale care decides whether a customer ever comes back. With acquisition costs rising every year, the cheapest growth most stores can find is keeping the customers they already paid to acquire.

The stakes show up in a few concrete ways:

  • Conversion. Buyers with an unanswered question about shipping, sizing or stock often abandon the cart instead of waiting.
  • Repeat purchase rate. A smooth return or a fast resolution is what earns a second and third order.
  • Refund and chargeback volume. Clear, quick communication heads off disputes before they reach your payment processor.
  • Team cost. Without automation, ticket volume grows in lockstep with sales, and headcount becomes your biggest support expense.
  • Brand reputation. Public reviews are shaped by how your store handles its worst support moments, not its best.

What to look for in a Shopify customer service app

The category is crowded, and the apps differ more than their landing pages suggest. A few criteria separate a tool that fits your store from one you will outgrow.

  • Shopify data depth. The best apps show order status, tracking and customer history inside the ticket, and let agents refund or edit orders without leaving the conversation.
  • AI automation. Modern support AI should train on your policies and catalog, resolve repetitive questions on its own and escalate cleanly when it cannot.
  • Channel coverage. Decide whether you need email and website chat only, or also WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger in one shared inbox.
  • Self-service. A help center or FAQ bot deflects a large share of tickets before a customer ever contacts you.
  • Pricing model. Per-agent pricing rewards tiny teams but scales expensively. Conversation-based or flat pricing can be cheaper as you grow.

The 12 best Shopify customer service apps

Here are the 12 apps, in order, with what each does well and who it suits.

#1 Gorgias, a helpdesk built for online stores

Gorgias is a helpdesk built for online stores. It centralizes email, live chat and social messages into a single inbox, with order data and customer history visible inside every ticket, and agents can refund, cancel or edit orders without leaving the conversation. Its automation rules and AI Agent can tag, answer and close repetitive tickets, and its listing also describes an AI shopping assistant that recommends products. Plans are priced by ticket volume: Starter costs $10/month for 50 tickets and three agents, and Basic $60/month for 300 tickets, with 7-day trials. Our guide to Gorgias pricing details the plans, ticket allowances and overages. Rated 4.3/5 from 705 reviews (October 7, 2026), Gorgias ranks first here for a full support operation, and Kanal's Gorgias integration adds WhatsApp as a channel inside it.

#2 Kanal WhatsApp, customer service on WhatsApp next to your helpdesk

Kanal ranks second because it handles customer service on one channel, WhatsApp, where the conversation stays on the customer's phone instead of ending with a closed browser tab. Its AI chatbot answers common questions automatically, from order tracking to product details, with live order status pulled from Shopify, while proactive order notifications answer the "where is my order" question before it becomes a ticket. When a conversation needs a person, it routes to your team with its context in a shared inbox, and integrations with Gorgias, Zendesk and Crisp keep WhatsApp inside the helpdesk you already run. The same inbox also serves abandoned cart recovery and campaigns. Kanal works on WhatsApp only: email and website chat stay in your helpdesk. Rated 5/5 from 125 reviews on the Shopify App Store (October 7, 2026), Kanal starts at $89/month, with a demo on request. The AI chatbot is not part of the entry plan: it comes with the Scale plan.

#3 Zendesk, enterprise-grade support infrastructure

Zendesk is an omnichannel platform built for large and complex support operations. It brings messaging, chat, email, phone and social into one workspace, with SLA tracking, reporting and team management tools, and its Shopify app shows customer and order data in the ticket sidebar, where agents can process refunds and cancellations. Pricing starts at $55 per agent per month on Suite Team (billed annually) and rises to $89, $115 and $150 per agent on the higher Suite tiers, with a 14-day free trial. Rated 3.2/5 from 93 reviews on its Shopify listing (October 7, 2026), Zendesk is built for brands processing thousands of tickets a month, and Kanal's Zendesk integration brings WhatsApp conversations into the same queue. To weigh per-agent against per-ticket billing, read our comparison of Zendesk and Gorgias.

#4 Re:amaze, helpdesk, chat and FAQ in one

Re:amaze combines a helpdesk, live chat and a knowledge base into a single platform designed around e-commerce. Its FAQ bot surfaces relevant help articles inside the chat widget before a customer finishes typing, which reduces ticket volume at the source. It covers chat, email, social and SMS, giving smaller teams broad channel reach without stitching several tools together, and the Shopify integration lets agents act on orders from the conversation. The Basic plan starts at $29/month, plus $29 per additional staff user, with a 14-day free trial. Rated 4.4/5 from 181 reviews, Re:amaze is built for stores that want a capable all-in-one without enterprise complexity, and multi-store support helps brands running several storefronts.

#5 Tidio, AI chat and helpdesk for growing stores

Tidio pairs Lyro, its AI agent, with a live chat widget and a helpdesk for chat, email, Instagram and WhatsApp in one inbox. Its listing describes Lyro answering questions and acting on orders within your rules (order status, refunds, cancellations and order edits), and starting conversations on product pages to compare products and add them to the cart. When a person steps in, the team sees the customer's orders and live cart. There is a free plan (live chat conversations with 50 users); the Customer Service and Flows plans start at $29/month, and the Lyro AI Chatbot plan at $39/month for up to 200 Lyro conversations, with 7-day trials. Rated 4.8/5 from 1,352 reviews, Tidio is built for small and mid-size stores that want AI chat first.

#6 Richpanel, AI support with a self-service focus

Richpanel is an AI-first customer support platform that leans hard on deflection. Its self-service portal lets customers track orders, start returns and resolve common issues themselves before ever opening a ticket, which keeps live volume down. Agents work from a unified inbox spanning email, chat and social, with Shopify order data attached to each conversation, and the AI drafts replies from your policies. There is no free plan: the PRO plan costs $89/month (plus $89 per additional user, with AI conversations from $0.20 each) after a 14-day free trial, and PRO MAX costs $119/month. For stores that want to scale support without scaling headcount, Richpanel's automation-heavy approach is a sensible choice, and its reporting shows how much volume the portal absorbs.

#7 Help Scout, a clean platform for human-led support

Help Scout is an all-in-one support platform known for a simple, uncluttered interface that teams adopt quickly. It combines a shared inbox, live chat and a Docs knowledge base, with AI assist features that draft and summarize replies. The Shopify integration shows order details, fulfillment and tracking in the customer sidebar, and lets agents refund or cancel recent orders. A free plan covers one shared email inbox and one public knowledge base with no AI tools, and the Standard plan at $30/month adds multiple inboxes, live chat, Instagram, Messenger and the AI inbox assistant, with a 15-day free trial. Help Scout suits brands that value a human, conversational support style over heavy automation, and its reporting on happiness scores and response times keeps a small team honest.

#8 eDesk, support built for multichannel sellers

eDesk is a helpdesk aimed at merchants who sell across many channels, not just their Shopify store. It centralizes conversations from your store and 300-plus marketplaces into one intelligent inbox, with order details on a single screen. AI templates and an AI knowledge base speed up responses to common queries, and agents can action returns, cancellations and replacement orders directly. Pricing starts at $59/month (Starter, one online store or marketplace) after a 14-day free trial, with Growth at $199/month and Professional at $399/month. For stores expanding onto Amazon and eBay, eDesk keeps support unified and protects marketplace metrics such as response time, which affect your listings' visibility.

#9 Willdesk, an affordable all-in-one helpdesk

Willdesk by Channelwill brings email, live chat, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp into one ticketing inbox at a low entry price. Its GPT-4 powered AI agent handles order tracking, returns, cancellations and address updates, and a workflow builder adds cart recovery and instant FAQ responses. A branded help center gives customers a self-service path, and Shopify order data sits inside every ticket. The free plan suits small stores, with paid plans from $16.90/month. Willdesk is a strong value pick for small and mid-size stores that want broad features without enterprise pricing.

#10 Gladly, people-centered support for premium brands

Gladly organizes support around the customer rather than the ticket, so every conversation across voice, chat and social stays in one lifelong thread. Its Sidekick AI handles routine requests and assists agents, while self-service threads let customers resolve issues on their own. The model fits brands that treat support as a relationship rather than a queue to clear, and the Shopify integration keeps order history in that thread. Gladly offers a free plan with 100 AI interactions and one team seat to test its AI agent on chat and email, then pay-as-you-go pricing on Starter ($1.50 per AI resolution, $0.25 per AI assist) with a monthly limit you set, capped at $250/month. It is best for established stores with high customer lifetime value and a support team large enough to use its routing.

#11 SB Helpdesk, FAQ-first support on a budget

SB Helpdesk focuses on self-service, pairing a help center and AI-powered FAQ pages with a ticketing system and live chat. Customers find answers in branded help center pages before opening a ticket, which deflects a meaningful share of repetitive questions. It connects email, chat and social into one inbox, and the entry pricing is among the lowest on this list, with a free plan (10 tickets a month, then $0.25 per ticket) and paid plans from $5.99/month. For small stores that want a polished FAQ and basic ticketing without much spend, it is a practical starting point that can be replaced later without a painful migration.

#12 MX Helpdesk, simple ticketing and live chat

MX Helpdesk is a straightforward all-in-one tool that combines a helpdesk, email ticketing and live chat for stores that want the essentials done cleanly. It centralizes email and chat conversations in one place, adds a FAQ widget for self-service and uses automation to cut repetitive work. The interface is easy to learn, and it is free, with no paid plan listed, which keeps it accessible. MX Helpdesk suits small teams that need reliable support tooling without a large budget, and it is a sensible first helpdesk for a store moving off a shared Gmail inbox.

Helpdesk vs live chat vs AI agent: what your store needs

These three terms get used loosely, but they solve different problems. A helpdesk tracks tickets across channels, live chat handles real-time website conversations, and an AI agent resolves questions automatically. Most stores end up wanting a blend, and the right mix depends on volume and team size.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best customer service app for Shopify?

Gorgias ranks first in this comparison: a helpdesk built for online stores, with order actions inside every ticket and plans priced by ticket volume from $10/month. Kanal ranks second, for stores whose customers write on WhatsApp: its AI chatbot answers with live order data. Tidio is built for small stores that want AI chat, and Zendesk for large support teams.

What is the difference between a customer service app and a live chat app?

A live chat app focuses on real-time conversations through a website widget. A customer service app is broader: it usually includes a helpdesk for ticketing, multiple channels, automation and often a knowledge base. Many apps, including most on this list, do both, but a dedicated live chat app is narrower in scope. If a chat widget is all you need, our Shopify Inbox guide covers Shopify's own chat app.

Can an AI agent fully replace my support team?

Not yet. AI agents handle repetitive, well-defined questions such as order tracking and FAQ lookups extremely well, and that covers a large share of typical ticket volume. But complex complaints, emotional situations and high-value customers still need a human. The most effective setup is a hybrid where AI handles first contact and escalates cleanly.

How much should a Shopify store budget for a customer service app?

It depends on volume and team size. Small stores can run a capable FAQ and ticketing setup for under $20 a month, while a full helpdesk with AI costs more: compare the entry prices in the table above. Watch the pricing model closely: per-agent plans look cheap at first but scale faster than conversation-based or flat pricing.

Which customer service app is best for a small Shopify store?

For a tight budget, SB Helpdesk and MX Helpdesk offer real ticketing and self-service at a low entry price. Willdesk gives broader features and a GPT-4 agent for slightly more. If your customers prefer messaging, Kanal handles support over WhatsApp while doubling as a sales channel, which makes the spend easier to justify.

Can I combine a helpdesk with a WhatsApp app?

Yes, and it is the setup most growing stores land on. The helpdesk owns email and website chat, and a WhatsApp app owns the messaging channel, with an integration so agents see everything in one queue. Kanal integrates natively with Gorgias, Zendesk and Crisp, so WhatsApp conversations appear as tickets while cart recovery and campaigns keep running from Kanal.

Do customer service apps slow down a Shopify store?

Only the chat widget touches your storefront; ticketing and AI run on the vendor's servers. Most widgets load asynchronously and add little weight, but test with Lighthouse on a mobile profile before and after install. WhatsApp-based support adds only a click-to-chat button, which adds very little to the page.

Our recommendation

There is no single best customer service app, only the best fit for your store's channels and volume. Gorgias, at the top of this comparison, is our first pick for a full support operation. If your customers reach you mostly by email, a focused helpdesk such as Gorgias or Help Scout will serve you well, and high-volume brands should look at Zendesk or Gladly. For stores on a tight budget, Willdesk, SB Helpdesk and MX Helpdesk deliver real value without enterprise pricing. If your customers already write to you on WhatsApp, add Kanal: it answers questions with an AI chatbot, replies with live order status, and uses the same conversation for cart recovery and campaigns. Whichever you pick, start with one tool, measure its effect on conversion, and expand from there.
