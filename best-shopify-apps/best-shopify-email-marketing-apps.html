How we compared the 9 apps

We connected each app to a test Shopify store with a 2,000-contact list, built the same three flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase), sent one campaign to a segment of past buyers, and checked inbox placement on Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail with seed addresses. We scored six dimensions: flow builder depth (branching, conditional splits, A/B tests), segmentation on Shopify data, multichannel options (SMS, push, WhatsApp integrations), deliverability and authentication support, editor and template quality, and price at 5,000 and 20,000 contacts. Pricing was read from the Shopify App Store in September 2026, and the listings of Klaviyo, Omnisend, Shopify Messaging, Mailchimp, Drip and Kanal were read again on October 7, 2026. We excluded apps with fewer than fifty Shopify reviews and tools without a meaningful update in the last twelve months.

Why email marketing still drives Shopify revenue

Email is not flashy, but it consistently outperforms other channels on return per dollar spent. For most Shopify stores, automated flows alone account for a large share of total email revenue while requiring almost no ongoing effort once they are set up.

  • It is an owned channel. Your list is yours. You are not renting attention from an ad network that can change pricing or targeting rules overnight.
  • Automation does the heavy lifting. Welcome, browse abandonment, abandoned cart and post-purchase flows run continuously and capture revenue you would otherwise lose.
  • Low cost per send. Email is one of the cheapest ways to reach a customer, which makes it ideal for newsletters, education and longer-form content.
  • Deep personalization. Modern platforms pull purchase history, browsing behavior and predictive data to make every send relevant.
  • Clear attribution. Good apps tie revenue back to specific flows and campaigns, so you can see exactly what is working.

The catch is that inboxes are crowded, and an urgent email can go unnoticed. That is why many merchants now pair email with a higher-engagement channel for time-sensitive messages, a point we return to below.

What to look for in a Shopify email marketing app

Picking an email app is not only about the monthly price. A cheap tool with weak deliverability or shallow automation can quietly cost you far more in lost revenue. Here are the criteria that matter most.

  • Automation depth. Look for a visual flow builder with branching logic, conditional splits and smart delays. Multi-step sequences consistently outperform single-email reminders.
  • Segmentation. Your app should segment on purchase history, order value, tags, browsing behavior and predicted lifetime value. Better segmentation means more relevant sends and lower unsubscribe rates.
  • Shopify data sync. Real-time sync of orders, customers and abandoned checkouts is essential. Without it, your messages go stale fast.
  • Deliverability. Strong sender reputation and authentication support (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) keep your emails out of spam. Ask any vendor about their inbox placement rates.
  • Pricing model. Most apps charge by contact count or email volume. Model your real list size as it grows, because a plan that is cheap at 1,000 contacts can get expensive at 20,000.
  • Multichannel reach. Many apps now bundle SMS, push or messaging channels. Consolidating channels in one tool simplifies reporting and often lowers total cost.
  • Templates and editor. A drag-and-drop editor with responsive, on-brand templates saves hours on every campaign.

The 9 best Shopify email marketing apps

#1 Klaviyo, segmentation and flows on Shopify data

Klaviyo is an email and SMS marketing platform built around customer data. Its Shopify listing describes real-time data from Shopify and more than 350 apps, AI-powered segments and flow triggers, A/B testing, predictive analytics and personalized email, SMS, WhatsApp, mobile push and pop-ups. The flow builder covers welcome, abandoned cart and winback sequences. Email is free up to 250 contacts, then starts at $20/month for 251 to 500 contacts and rises with the size of the list: our Klaviyo pricing guide walks through the tiers. Rated 4.7/5 from 3,358 reviews on the Shopify App Store (October 7, 2026), Klaviyo ranks first here for stores that want to drive email from purchase and browsing data, and Kanal's Klaviyo integration triggers WhatsApp messages from the same flows.

#2 Omnisend, email, SMS and web push in one tool

Omnisend is built for e-commerce and combines email, SMS and web push in one platform, with pre-built welcome, abandoned cart and post-purchase workflows, plus popups and signup forms for list growth. Its listing also describes AI product recommendations and campaigns built from ChatGPT or Claude. The free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 emails a month; Standard starts at $16/month for up to 500 contacts and Pro at $59/month for up to 2,500, and both rise with the size of the list. Rated 4.7/5 from 3,186 reviews, Omnisend is built for stores that want several channels in one tool without assembling every flow from scratch. To see how the two bills grow with your list, read our comparison of Omnisend and Klaviyo pricing.

#3 Shopify Messaging (formerly Shopify Email), the native option in the admin

Shopify Messaging is the new name of Shopify Email, Shopify's own marketing app, built into the admin you already use. Its listing now covers email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns created, sent and tracked from Shopify, with ready-made templates, customer segments, email and SMS automations, and Sidekick to adapt emails to your brand. The first 10,000 emails of each month are included, then pricing starts at $1 per 1,000 emails. Rated 4.8/5 from 4,608 reviews, it is built for new stores and for merchants who send occasional campaigns and want to stay inside Shopify.

#4 Mailchimp, a familiar all-rounder

Mailchimp is a general-purpose email platform whose Shopify app syncs customers, products and orders. Its listing describes automated welcome, abandoned cart and back-in-stock emails, segments built from Shopify customer events, single-use discount codes, pop-up forms and revenue attribution inside Shopify, with SMS as an add-on. There is a free plan; Essentials starts at $13/month, Standard at $20/month and Premium at $350/month, all priced by number of contacts. Rated 4.8/5 from 1,507 reviews, Mailchimp is built for merchants who already know the interface or whose marketing reaches beyond Shopify. If Klaviyo is the other name on your shortlist, see our side-by-side look at Klaviyo and Mailchimp.

#5 Drip, built for e-commerce personalization

Drip is an email automation and popup app for Shopify stores. Its listing describes pre-built segments drawn from Shopify customer data, flow templates for abandoned cart, back-in-stock, price drop and welcome emails, a workflow builder with A/B split testing, and quizzes, forms and popups for list growth. It can also be driven from Claude or ChatGPT. The subscription starts at $39/month with a 14-day trial and rises with the number of active people on your list and the volume of emails you send. Rated 4.5/5 from 55 reviews, Drip is built for stores that want behavior-based email and popups in one app.

#6 AVADA Email Marketing, budget-friendly automation

AVADA Email Marketing offers a surprising amount of capability for its price. It includes automation series, customer groups, a drag-and-drop editor, popups for list growth and an SMS option, with a free plan and paid plans from $16/month. It will not match the segmentation depth of Klaviyo or Drip, but for small and mid-sized stores that want functional automation without a steep bill, AVADA delivers solid value. It is a good pick for merchants graduating from Shopify Messaging who are not ready for a premium platform, and the bundled popups save a separate list-growth app.

#7 Privy, list growth plus email in one tool

Privy started as a popup and list-growth tool and later added email and SMS, which is still where its strength lies. If capturing email addresses through well-targeted popups, spin-to-win forms and announcement bars is a priority, Privy bundles that with email automations so you do not need a separate signup app. The email side covers the essentials, including welcome and abandoned cart flows, without the advanced segmentation of dedicated platforms. There is no free plan: pop-ups start at $24/month for up to 10,000 monthly pageviews, and email starts at $30/month for up to 1,500 contacts. Privy is best for stores focused on growing the list as much as emailing it.

#8 Seguno, the Shopify-native simple choice

Seguno is built to live inside the Shopify admin, with no separate login or external dashboard. That tight integration makes it one of the simplest email tools to run: you work with native Shopify customer segments and a clean editor without leaving the platform, and product blocks pull straight from your catalog. It covers automated emails and newsletters well but is email only, with no SMS. It is free up to 250 subscribers, and the Standard plan at $35/month covers 2,500 subscribers and adds popups, reviews, banners and back-in-stock alerts. Seguno suits merchants who value simplicity and a native experience over advanced features, and it is a natural upgrade from Shopify Messaging.

#9 Recart, managed multichannel for larger brands

Recart focuses on done-for-you messaging, with a heavy emphasis on SMS alongside other direct channels. Pre-built flows and managed onboarding mean a lot of the setup is handled for you, and a service team writes and optimizes campaigns, which appeals to brands that want results without building everything in-house. At $299/month with no free plan, Recart sits firmly at the premium end. It is best suited to larger Shopify stores with sizable audiences that value a hands-off, managed approach and can justify the fee with the revenue those flows return.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best email marketing app for Shopify?

Klaviyo ranks first in this comparison for its segmentation and flow builder on Shopify data, and Omnisend second for stores that want email, SMS and web push in one tool. New stores can start at no cost with Shopify Messaging (formerly Shopify Email) or Seguno. Kanal does not send email: it adds WhatsApp next to any of these apps, with a Klaviyo integration if that is your platform.

How much does a Shopify email marketing app cost?

Costs range from free to several hundred dollars a month. Shopify Messaging and Seguno start free, mid-range platforms such as Omnisend and AVADA start at $16/month, and managed services such as Recart start at $299/month. Most apps bill by contact count or email volume, so model your list as it grows rather than pricing only the starting tier.

Which email marketing app is best for a new Shopify store?

For a brand-new store, Shopify Messaging (formerly Shopify Email) is a no-cost starting point built into the admin. As you grow and want real automation flows, Omnisend and AVADA offer strong value at accessible prices. Klaviyo is the natural upgrade once data-led segmentation becomes a priority.

Do I still need email if I use WhatsApp or SMS?

Yes. Email is the lowest-cost channel and the best for newsletters, education and longer-form content, and your list is an owned asset. WhatsApp and SMS shine for time-sensitive, high-engagement messages. The strongest setup uses email for depth and a messaging channel for urgency, not one instead of the other.

Can these apps recover abandoned carts automatically?

Most apps on this list include abandoned cart automation that triggers when a shopper leaves without buying. The flows differ in sophistication: Klaviyo and Drip offer advanced branching, while simpler tools send a basic reminder. For higher recovery rates, many merchants add WhatsApp cart reminders, which you can read about on our abandoned cart recovery page and in the best Shopify abandoned cart apps guide.

Can I run two email marketing apps at the same time?

You can, but it is rarely worth it. Two apps means two consent lists, two sender reputations and a real risk of sending the same customer the same flow twice. Pick one email platform and add different channels around it instead: Kanal for WhatsApp, a popup tool for list growth if your email app lacks one, and Reviewz.ai for review requests.

Do these apps support Shopify Markets and multiple languages?

Most established platforms support multiple languages in templates and flows, and Klaviyo, Omnisend and Drip can segment on the customer's country and currency from Shopify Markets so each market receives localized sends. Check whether the app translates transactional-style automations automatically or requires a flow per language, and whether it respects per-market consent rules under GDPR.

Our recommendation

For most growing Shopify stores, Klaviyo is our first pick for its segmentation and automation on Shopify data, with Omnisend a close runner-up that covers email, SMS and web push in one tool.
