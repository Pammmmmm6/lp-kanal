A Shopify subscription app sets up recurring orders, gives subscribers a portal to manage them and retries failed payments. Launching a subscription is the easy part. Keeping subscribers past the third renewal is harder, when failed cards, forgotten deliveries and unread notices quietly erode the base.

That is why this guide looks at retention as much as billing. We compared 11 Shopify subscription apps on subscription types, customer portal, dunning, transaction fees, analytics and migration support. The range goes from free plans for a first replenishment offer to platforms built for large subscription programs. Two tools are featured outside the ranking: Kanal, the WhatsApp layer that works alongside your subscription app through its Recharge integration, and Reviewz.ai for review requests.

How we compared the 11 apps

We installed each app on a test Shopify store and created a replenishment subscription with a 30-day interval, a prepaid three-month plan and, where supported, a build-a-box offer. We then ran renewals with a failing test card to observe dunning. We scored six dimensions: subscription types supported, customer portal (skip, pause, swap, address and card updates without contacting support), dunning and churn tools, analytics depth, transaction fees on top of the monthly price, and migration or onboarding help. Prices, plan limits and features were checked on each app's Shopify App Store listing on September 10, 2026. Apps no longer available on the App Store were removed, and the apps featured outside the ranking are labeled as such.

Subscription models that work on Shopify

  • Replenishment. The same consumable delivered on a fixed interval: coffee, supplements, pet food, razors. The simplest model and the easiest to automate.
  • Curated box. A selection chosen by the brand each cycle. Higher perceived value, more operational work, and the model most exposed to churn when a box disappoints.
  • Build-a-box. The subscriber picks the contents each cycle. It retains best when the portal makes swapping effortless, and worst when it does not.
  • Access and membership. A recurring fee for content, discounts or early access, often paired with physical products.
  • Prepaid. Several cycles paid upfront at a discount. Lower churn by construction and better cash flow, at the cost of a bigger first commitment.

What to look for in a Shopify subscription app

  • Checkout. Prefer apps that sell subscriptions through Shopify Checkout and Shop Pay rather than an external checkout. The "Works with" section of each listing tells you.
  • Customer portal. Skip, pause, swap, reschedule and update a card without emailing support. Every action that requires a ticket risks becoming a cancellation.
  • Dunning. Automatic card retries, update-your-card reminders and a grace period help recover some of the failed renewals.
  • Fees. Compare the monthly price plus the percentage plus the per-transaction amount at the subscription revenue you expect in six months, not today.
  • Analytics. Cohorts, churn and lifetime value matter more than a revenue total once the program grows.
  • Migration. If you may switch later, prefer platforms with assisted migration and exportable contracts.
  • Messaging integrations. Renewal and dunning messages need a channel that gets read. Check Klaviyo support, and whether your WhatsApp tool connects to the app: Kanal integrates with Recharge.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Shopify subscription app?

Easy Subscription App, #1 in our ranking, is the best subscription app for most Shopify stores: subscribe and save, prepaid, boxes and bundles, 0% transaction fees on every plan and a free plan up to 100 subscription orders. The other Shopify subscription apps on this list differ mainly on fees and limits. Seal also charges 0% transaction fees on every plan, including a free plan for 50 subscriptions. Appstle is free up to $500 a month in subscriptions. Recharge, Skio and Loop add cancel flows and retention analytics, with a percentage fee per transaction on their main paid plans. If you pick Recharge, Kanal adds renewal reminders, failed-payment recovery and win-back offers on WhatsApp, from $89/month (the Recharge integration comes with the Scale plan).

Are there free subscription apps for Shopify?

Yes, with limits. Seal is free for 50 subscriptions, Loop for 50 active subscriptions, Easy Subscriptions up to 100 subscription orders, and Appstle up to $500 a month in subscriptions. PayWhirl has no monthly fee but takes 3% after the first $5,000 processed. Subi and Recurpay reserve their free plans for development stores. Check subscriber caps and whether dunning is included before you launch.

Monthly fee or transaction fee: which is cheaper?

It depends on your subscription revenue. A 1% fee on $5,000 of monthly subscription sales is $50, less than many fixed plans. At $50,000 it becomes $500, and a plan with 0% transaction fees can cost less. Model both at your expected volume six months out, and remember that several apps charge a fixed monthly price plus a percentage plus a per-transaction amount.

Do subscription apps work with Shopify checkout, Shopify Plus and Shopify Markets?

Most apps on this list name Shopify Checkout or Shop Pay among the tools they work with, so read the "Works with" section of each listing before you install. Portal translations and per-market shipping rules depend on the app. Seal, Easy Subscriptions and Subi list many interface languages, and Loop's Pro plan adds a multilingual customer experience. Test a renewal in each market and currency before launch.

Can I switch subscription apps without losing subscribers?

Yes, but plan it carefully. Skio, Subi, Bold, PayWhirl, Appstle and Recurpay all mention migration help on their listings, and Bold's is complimentary. Communicate the change to subscribers before it happens, ideally on a channel they read, and freeze plan changes during the migration window.

How do I reduce subscription churn?

Start with passive churn: failed payments, deliveries that arrive too soon and renewals customers forgot about. Fix it with dunning that retries cards and asks for an update, a portal where skipping is easier than cancelling, and reminders sent where they are read. With its Recharge integration, Kanal sends upcoming-order reminders three days before the charge, failed-payment messages with a one-tap card update link and win-back offers on WhatsApp.
