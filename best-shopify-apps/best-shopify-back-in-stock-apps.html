When a product sells out, the demand does not disappear. Shoppers keep landing on the product page, and without a waitlist, nothing brings them back when the stock does. A back in stock app keeps that demand: the shopper joins a waitlist, and the app sends an alert when the product is available again. Every app in this list sends restock alerts. The differences are where the alert lands, whether you can also take pre-orders, and what each alert costs you.

What to look for in a back in stock app

A restock alert looks simple, but a few details decide how many waiting customers come back to buy.

  • The channel. Email is the default. SMS, web push and WhatsApp reach customers on their phone. Pick the channel your customers read, and check how each one is billed.
  • Signup friction. A Notify Me form asks for an email or a phone number. A WhatsApp opt-in takes one tap, with no form to fill in and no account to create.
  • Variants and locations. Alerts should follow the exact size or color the customer asked for and, if you run several warehouses, the stock of the location that ships it.
  • Pre-orders. If restocks take weeks, a pre-order option lets customers pay in full or leave a deposit before the stock lands.
  • Demand data. A waitlist is a forecast. Look for a report of how many customers wait for each product, so you know what to reorder first.
  • Limits and usage fees. Most plans cap alerts, emails or pre-orders per month, and SMS is billed per message on top. Price a restock of your best seller before you choose a plan.
  • Integrations. If Klaviyo or another email tool runs your marketing, check that waitlist signups sync to it.

Back in stock alerts vs pre-orders

Alerts and pre-orders answer the same sold-out page in two ways. A back in stock alert collects a waitlist and messages it when inventory returns. The customer pays at that point, and you promise nothing until the stock is on the shelf. A pre-order sells the product before it arrives, in full or with a deposit. You get the order and the cash earlier, but you commit to a delivery date and have to keep customers informed if it moves.

Alerts fit products that come back regularly. Pre-orders fit launches, made-to-order items and long supplier lead times. Five apps in this list do both (Notify Me!, STOQ, Timesact, PreOrder Globo and Amp), and Swym adds pre-order alerts. Kanal focuses on the alert and on the conversation that follows it on WhatsApp, where a customer can reply with a question before buying.

Kbite adds a customizable "Notify me when available" button to out-of-stock products and sends automatic back in stock alerts by email and SMS. The same signup can build a price drop waitlist, so customers also hear when a product goes on sale, and the product analytics show which items are most in demand. Notify Me! adds Notify Me, PreOrder or Wishlist buttons to out-of-stock product, home and collection pages, then sends automatic restock alerts by email, SMS and web push. The free Lite plan includes 10 restock notifications, 5 pre-orders and 50 wishlist actions. Paid plans start at $9.90/month (Kickstart, 500 alerts and 500 pre-orders) and reach $39.90/month (Standard), with unlimited plans available inside the app. Rated 4.9/5 from 3,761 reviews and Built for Shopify, Notify Me! is worth a look if you want alerts, pre-orders and a wishlist without stacking three apps.

#4 STOQ, pre-orders with deposits and automatic restock alerts

STOQ, by Artos Software, puts a Preorder or Notify Me button on out-of-stock products, so each product can either take orders early or build a waitlist. Pre-orders cover presales and backorders with deposits or partial payments, including B2B pre-orders, and back in stock alerts go out automatically by email and SMS when you restock. It syncs your mailing list with Klaviyo, works with Mailchimp and Omnisend, supports multiple locations and markets, and reports on customer demand. The free plan includes 10 pre-orders and 30 emails a month. Paid plans cost $10/month (Lite), $29/month (Pro) and $69/month (Unlimited), with SMS at $0.025 each and a fee per pre-order above the plan limit ($0.25 on Lite, $0.10 on Pro). Support is available by live chat 24/7. Rated 5/5 from 3,678 reviews and Built for Shopify, STOQ is worth a look if you want to keep selling during a stockout instead of only waiting for it.

#5 Timesact, pre-orders and waitlists with flexible pricing

Timesact, listed as Preorder Now Presale Timesact, is a pre-order manager with waitlists and restock alerts built in. A product tag switches on pre-orders, back in stock alerts or a coming soon drop, and the inventory manager turns pre-orders and restock alerts on or off as stock levels change. Customers can pay with deposits, partial or split payments, and you can add automatic discounts, order limits, badges, shipping dates and a countdown timer. Restock alerts go out by email, with SMS on paid plans from $0.02 per message for US numbers. The free plan for new users covers 20 orders a month with unlimited emails and Klaviyo. CORE Flex starts at $5/month, and fixed plans with unlimited orders start at $23/month on Basic Shopify, priced by Shopify plan. Rated 4.9/5 from 2,063 reviews and Built for Shopify, Timesact is worth a look if pre-orders drive your launches.

Orders are tagged automatically, for example with "preorder" or a shipping date. Rated 4.9/5 from 1,921 reviews and Built for Shopify, PreOrder Globo is worth a look if you want pre-orders and email alerts at a low fixed price.

Shoppers join a waitlist through a signup form, and alerts and back order reminders go out by email and SMS. Forms and notifications are fully customizable and work with all themes, and the reports show customer demand for new and existing products. It works with Klaviyo, Mailchimp, PushOwl, Wiser and Zapier. Pre-orders are not part of the listing. The free plan includes 10 emails a month. Starter costs $19.99/month (300 emails, Klaviyo and Mailchimp integration), Pro $29.99/month (1,000 emails, collection page integration) and Premium $49.99/month (3,000 emails, countdown timer), and messages are billed from $0.05 to $0.40 each depending on their length. Rated 4.7/5 from 1,458 reviews, Appikon is worth a look if you only need restock alerts and demand reports.

#9 Swym Back in Stock Alerts, restock alerts that feed your other tools

Swym Back in Stock Alerts, by Swym Corporation, adds a Notify me button to out-of-stock products and sends automatic restock alerts and reminders by email and SMS. Its focus is the rest of your stack: out-of-stock subscribers can join your email lists and receive alerts through your email or SMS platform, with integrations including Klaviyo, Omnisend, Mailchimp, Listrak and Postscript, and you can run Facebook and Instagram retargeting ads to shoppers interested in a product. It also supports coming soon alerts, pre-order alerts, batch sending, Shopify Locations and AI-powered in-stock recommendations for out-of-stock products. There is no free plan: Starter costs $19.99/month (up to 1,000 alert requests), Pro $59.99/month (up to 10,000) and Premium $99.99/month (up to 25,000, with multi-location support and a REST API), each with a 30-day free trial. Rated 4.7/5 from 442 reviews, Swym is worth a look if your restock alerts need to run through an existing email tool or ad audience.

Klaviyo is an email and SMS marketing platform rather than a dedicated alert app, but its Shopify listing includes back-in-stock emails among its campaign types, next to price drop, browse abandonment and win-back emails. Rated 4.7/5 from 3,288 reviews, Klaviyo is worth a look if you want restock emails managed with your other campaigns.
