What to look for in a Shopify popup app

A popup can grow your list or get in the way of the purchase. These points make the difference.

  • The contact you want. Email is the default. SMS needs a phone number and SMS consent, and WhatsApp needs an opt-in for that channel. Pick the channel you will actually use to follow up, and check how the popup records consent. Our guide to WhatsApp opt-in rules covers the consent wording for a WhatsApp popup.
  • Formats. A simple email form is enough to start. Spin-to-win wheels, quizzes, smart bars and embedded forms give you other ways to present the same offer.
  • Timing and targeting. Look for triggers (delay, scroll, exit intent) and rules by page, device, traffic source or location, so each visitor sees an offer that fits the visit.
  • A/B testing. Testing the offer, the headline or the format is how a popup improves over time, and how you see its effect on your conversion rate. Nine of the eleven popup apps in this list include it, and Alia and Wisepops go further with AI-run tests and control groups.
  • Where the contacts go. Check that signups reach the tool that sends your follow-up (Klaviyo, Omnisend, Mailchimp or Shopify Email, for example), with the consent and the discount code attached.
  • Pricing model. Plans grow with pageviews (OptiMonk, Privy, Wisepops), visitors (Poptin, Justuno, Alia), form submissions (POWR), new subscribers (SendWILL), contacts (Klaviyo, Omnisend) or your Shopify plan (Pop Convert). Price your busiest month, not an average one.
  • Speed and support. A popup loads on every page. Pop Convert cites a load time under 50 ms on its listing, and several apps, including Pop Convert, Alia and Omnisend, list 24/7 live chat support.

How to create a pop-up in Shopify

Shopify has its own option, the Shopify Forms app, which adds popup and inline forms to your store. Here is how to publish a popup with it, following Shopify's Help Center:

  1. Install Shopify Forms from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Turn on the Forms app embed: in your Shopify admin, go to Online Store, click Edit theme, open App embeds, activate the Forms toggle and click Save.
  3. Go to Apps > Forms, click Create form, select Popup form and give the form a name.
  4. Choose the display: Floating, with a position on your store, or Overlay, in the middle of the page.
  5. In the form settings, write the title and text, pick the fields and choose the marketing consent: email, SMS or WhatsApp, with the phone field required for SMS and WhatsApp consent.
  6. Set the teaser, the success message (which can show a discount code), the styling, the image and the behavior: the devices, the pages (for example only product or collection pages) and the trigger, on the first, second or third page view, immediately or after a delay, or when a desktop visitor is about to leave the page.
  7. Click Save, preview the form and click Turn form on. New forms stay inactive until you do.

Shopify Forms has limits: up to 25 forms per store, and no multi-step forms or conditional logic. Popup apps follow the same logic from their own editor and add formats, targeting rules and, on most of them, A/B tests. On WhatsApp, collecting consent is only half the job: WhatsApp marketing is allowed through the official WhatsApp Business API, to customers who opted in. Kanal runs on that API, collects WhatsApp opt-ins at checkout and on your site, and sends campaigns and automated flows to those contacts.

The 11 best Shopify popup apps

#1 Pop Convert, unlimited popups and smart bars on a Shopify-plan price

Pop Convert is an all-in-one popup app: email and phone number capture forms, smart bars, rolling banners, spin wheels and social proof popups that blend into your Shopify theme. Popups can trigger on exit intent, geolocation or specific page URLs, you can A/B test several versions of a campaign, and more than 250 templates give you a starting point. The listing cites a load time under 50 ms and 24/7 live chat support, and the app works with Shopify Flow. Beyond capture, its feature list covers email campaigns and welcome, abandoned cart and win-back emails. The free plan covers 1,000 unique visitors and 50 subscribers a month. The Unlimited plan is priced by Shopify plan: $15/month on Basic, $29 on Grow, $79 on Advanced and $129 on Plus, for up to 5 stores, with unlimited visitors, popups and emails and a 90-day free trial. Rated 4.9/5 from 8,718 reviews and Built for Shopify, Pop Convert is worth a look if you want unlimited popups at a fixed monthly price.

#2 SendWILL, email and SMS popups with email marketing built in

SendWILL, listed on the App Store as CWILL Popup Email Marketing and previously known as EcomSend, pairs list-building popups with email marketing. You capture subscribers with email or SMS popups, exit-intent forms, newsletter signup forms and spin-the-wheel offers, often with a discount, then email them with campaigns from a drag-and-drop editor and with welcome, abandoned cart, win-back and post-purchase automations. The app works with Shopify Flow, Klaviyo, Mailchimp, SMSBump and Privy. A/B testing is not part of the listing. The free plan includes 30 new subscribers and 2,000 marketing emails a month, with unlimited popup views and stored contacts. Paid plans cost $11.90/month (Essential, 150 new subscribers and 5,000 emails), $29.90/month (Pro, 500 and 20,000) and $79.90/month (Premium, 2,000 and 60,000), each with a 14-day free trial, and usage charges may apply beyond the limits. Rated 4.9/5 from 7,713 reviews and Built for Shopify, SendWILL is worth a look if you want popups and email in one low-cost app.

#3 Privy, popups with email and SMS from one vendor

Privy grows your email and SMS lists with dozens of popup display options, then sends the emails and texts itself. The Pop Ups & Displays plan includes unlimited popups and displays, every popup type, advanced targeting, multi-step and mini quiz forms, templates and a sync with third-party tools, and Privy's help center shows how to A/B test a display to see which version captures the most signups. On the email side you get a drag-and-drop editor, a flow builder and revenue reporting, and SMS adds after-signup and cart saver texts. It works with Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Attentive, Postscript, Emotive and Smile.io. There is no free plan on the listing: Pop Ups & Displays starts at $24/month for up to 10,000 monthly pageviews, Email costs $30/month for up to 1,500 contacts, and Email and SMS costs $45/month with 1,250 SMS credits. Some charges may be billed outside your Shopify invoice. Rated 4.5/5 from 4,208 reviews, Privy is worth a look if you want popups, email and SMS in one platform.

#4 Justuno, AI popups with visitor identification and expert help

Justuno, listed as Pop-Ups & Spotlight by Justuno, presents itself as a list growth partner for retention marketers. Its AI popups capture email and SMS through quizzes, spin-to-win games, exit offers and banners, and AI Smart Timing shows each offer when a shopper is ready to act. Spotlight identifies anonymous visitors to feed your abandonment flows, and you can A/B test offers, design and messaging. Plans include flyouts, message bars, embedded promos, surveys and upsell or cross-sell offers, and a Justuno expert can take on strategy, design and optimization. It works with Klaviyo, Attentive, Postscript, Omnisend and Iterable. Pricing follows monthly unique visitors: $59/month for up to 10,000, $99 for 20,000 (with white glove onboarding), $199 for 50,000 and $299 for 100,000, each with a 14-day free trial, and larger plans are on Justuno's website. Rated 4.6/5 from 670 reviews, Justuno is worth a look if you want managed popups and visitor identification on higher traffic.

#5 OptiMonk, an AI popup builder with a free plan

OptiMonk is built around an AI agent: you describe your goal in a chat and give it your website URL, and it builds custom, on-brand popups and suggests targeting, triggers and A/B tests. Its feature list covers email, SMS, cart and sales popups, exit intent, spin-the-wheel and other games, surveys, quizzes and banners, with targeting, geolocation and segmentation. A/B testing compares headlines, offers or messages, the analytics focus on revenue rather than clicks or signups, and personalization shows different messages to different customers. It works with Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Attentive, Postscript and Google Analytics 4. The free plan includes 10,000 pageviews a month on one domain, with all features, unlimited campaigns and unlimited users. Paid plans cost $29/month (Essential, 20,000 pageviews, 2 domains, unbranded), $99/month (Growth, 100,000) and $249/month (Premium, 500,000, with a key account manager), and yearly billing lowers the price. Rated 4.8/5 from 459 reviews, OptiMonk is worth a look if you want AI help to design popups and room to test them before you pay.

POWR Popup, by POWR.io, keeps things simple: discount offers, newsletter signups and email capture popups built from more than 250 templates and 20+ elements such as countdown timers, images, GIFs and custom calls to action. Popups can appear immediately, on scroll, after a delay, on exit intent or with a custom trigger, and campaign scheduling handles limited-time offers and product releases. How do I create a pop-up in Shopify?

With Shopify's own Shopify Forms app: install it from the Shopify App Store, turn on the Forms app embed in your theme editor, then go to Apps > Forms, click Create form and select Popup form. Choose a floating or overlay display, set the fields, success message, styling and behavior (devices, pages and trigger), preview the form and click Turn form on. Popup apps work the same way from their own editor, with more formats, more targeting options and, on most of them, A/B tests. The full step list is in the How to Create a Pop-up in Shopify section above.
