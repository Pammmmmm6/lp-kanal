Shoppers ask questions before they buy, and many of them arrive at night, on weekends or mid-checkout. A Shopify chatbot app answers instantly: where is my order, does this run true to size, do you ship to Belgium. The best chatbots in 2026 read your catalog, policies and order data, recommend products, nudge abandoned carts and hand over to a human when judgment is needed.

We compared twelve of the best Shopify chatbot apps on AI answer quality, channels, human handoff and pricing, from WhatsApp-first AI to web chat widgets and full helpdesks.

We installed each app on a test Shopify store, connected catalog and order data, then asked the same twenty questions about products, sizing, shipping, order status and returns. We scored accuracy on store-specific questions, channels supported, handoff to a human with context preserved, actions the bot can take on its own, languages, and price at 1,000 monthly conversations. Ratings, review counts and prices were read from each Shopify App Store listing in September 2026. One candidate was dropped because its listing had no reviews.

Why a chatbot matters for a Shopify store

A question that goes unanswered is often a sale that goes elsewhere. A visitor who gets an answer while still on the page can buy right away, whereas one who waits for an email reply has time to look elsewhere. A chatbot solves three problems at once.

  • Coverage. It answers at 2 a.m., during launches and on Black Friday, when your team cannot scale.
  • Repetitive tickets. Order status, shipping times and return policies make up most inbound messages. Automating them frees agents for conversations that need a human.
  • Selling. Modern AI assistants recommend products, apply a discount and walk a shopper to checkout.

The channel matters as much as the bot: a website widget only reaches visitors while they are on the page, while a WhatsApp conversation stays open in the customer's phone.

What to look for in a Shopify chatbot app

  • Training on your store. The bot should read products, variants, stock, policies and order history, not a static FAQ.
  • Actions, not only answers. Order lookup, tracking links, add to cart, discount codes and cancellations are where the time savings are.
  • Human handoff. A shared inbox, escalation rules and a conversation summary when an agent takes over.
  • Channels and languages. Website chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or email, wherever your customers write, in the language they write in.
  • Pricing model. Per seat, per AI conversation or per visitor. Prefer tools that cap AI usage so the bill stays predictable.

The 12 best Shopify chatbot apps

#1 Tidio, Lyro AI agent with predictable costs

Tidio combines live chat, a ticketing inbox and Lyro, an AI agent that resolves support questions, recommends products, adds them to the cart and handles refunds, cancellations and order edits inside Shopify. Chat, email, Instagram and WhatsApp land in one inbox. AI usage is capped, so Lyro pauses at your limit. The free plan covers live chat with 50 users and flows for 100 visitors a month; Customer Service and Flows plans start at $29/month and Lyro AI at $39/month, with 7-day trials. Rated 4.8/5 from 1,338 reviews, Tidio is a safe first chatbot for stores that want web chat and AI in one tool.

#2 Kanal WhatsApp, the AI chatbot that answers on WhatsApp

Kanal puts an AI chatbot on WhatsApp rather than in a website widget, which is why it ranks second overall and first for stores whose customers already write on WhatsApp. The assistant is trained on your Shopify catalog, stock, policies and order data, so it answers product, shipping and order-status questions 24/7, in 96+ languages, and hands conversations that need judgment to your team in a shared inbox with the customer's Shopify context alongside. Because the same platform runs abandoned cart recovery and campaigns, the bot can close the sale it started rather than only deflect a ticket. Rated 5/5 on the Shopify App Store, Kanal starts at $89/month (Pro), with Scale at $149/month; the AI agent is not part of the entry plan. See the AI chatbot page and the WhatsApp for Shopify guide.

#3 Gorgias, AI agent inside a full helpdesk

Gorgias is a helpdesk first and a chatbot second. Its AI Agent resolves tickets end to end across email, chat, social media, voice and SMS from one unified inbox, and an AI Shopping Assistant recommends products and guides shoppers on the storefront. Analytics turn conversations into revenue insights, and orders and customer history sit next to every ticket. Plans start at $10/month (Starter) and scale through $60, $360 and $900/month, with a 7-day free trial. Rated 4.3/5 from 705 reviews, Gorgias suits brands that need workflows, macros and reporting around the bot. Kanal integrates with Gorgias, so WhatsApp conversations can flow into the same helpdesk.

#4 Shopify Inbox, the free AI sales agent

Shopify Inbox is free, built by Shopify and already connected to your store. Its AI sales associate helps buyers find products and answers catalog questions, and when a customer signs in with Shop, replies can use preferences and purchase history from across Shopify. You set the tone and rules, rate chats to train the agent, and track orders assisted and buyer satisfaction in a dashboard. The limits: it covers your online store chat, not WhatsApp, Instagram or email, and there is no ticketing layer. Rated 4.6/5 from 5,799 reviews, it is the obvious starting point before you pay for anything.

#5 Rep AI, behavioral AI for hesitating shoppers

Rep AI watches on-site behavior, spots a shopper who is hesitating and opens a chat to save the sale. The GPT-based assistant is trained on your catalog, policies and brand voice, recommends products, guides shoppers to checkout and handles order tracking, returns and FAQs 24/7. When a human is needed, it transitions to live chat with a full summary. Pricing follows traffic: free for 100 visitors a month, then Starter at $104/month, Basic at $209 and Standard at $368, with 14-day trials. Rated 4.6/5 from 114 reviews, Rep AI fits stores with healthy traffic that want a proactive sales rep rather than a bot that waits to be asked.

#6 Willdesk, GPT-4 replies with ticketing and a help center

Willdesk, by Channelwill, is a helpdesk with AI built in. Email, live chat, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp are unified in one ticketing system, GPT-4 powers instant FAQ answers and product recommendations, and a workflow builder handles ticket routing, cart recovery and task assignment. A branded help center with multi-language FAQ pages and integrations with TrackingMore, Shopify Flow and loyalty tools complete the package. The free plan is limited to 20 conversations, then Basic costs $16.90/month and Pro $89.90/month, with 7-day trials. Rated 4.8/5 from 387 reviews, Willdesk is one of the cheapest ways to get ticketing, a help center and AI replies in a single app.

#7 Chatway, AI agent and live chat with mobile apps

Chatway pairs a live chat widget with an AI agent that handles support and sales 24/7. The inbox shows Shopify cart, order and coupon data next to each conversation and unifies live chat, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs and email. Canned responses and an FAQ block speed up human agents, and iOS and Android apps let a small team answer from anywhere. The free plan is enough for a solo operator; Solo costs $29/month, Team $79 and Plus $149, with 14-day trials. Rated 4.9/5 from 281 reviews, Chatway suits small teams that want a polished widget, social channels and a mobile app without a heavy helpdesk.

#8 Manifest AI, ChatGPT assistant with quizzes and cart nudges

Manifest AI adds a ChatGPT-powered assistant that answers customer queries, runs a product quiz, recommends products and reduces cart drop-offs with real-time discount nudges. It positions itself as a sales tool as much as a support one, built to lift conversion and average order value while cutting tickets, and it can talk to shoppers in any language. The free plan lets you test it without commitment; paid plans are arranged through a demo rather than listed on the App Store, so expect a conversation about volume. Rated 4.7/5 from 60 reviews, Manifest AI suits stores with a large catalog where guided selling and quizzes help shoppers choose.

#9 Richpanel, AI agents that resolve tickets end to end

Richpanel is an AI-native helpdesk for Shopify brands. Its AI agents resolve order status, returns, refunds, cancellations and subscription tickets end to end, in your brand voice, while a Manager AI studies your policies and past tickets to write the playbooks the agents follow. You keep control with guardrails: refund limits, what needs approval and when to escalate. The unified inbox covers email, chat, social media and SMS. Plans start at $89/month (PRO), with PRO MAX and Self Service at $119/month, each with a 14-day trial. Rated 4.9/5 from 139 reviews, Richpanel is the pick for teams that want the bot to complete resolutions, not just answer questions.

#10 Crisp, twelve channels in one inbox

Crisp centralizes twelve channels, including email, WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Messenger and phone, in a single helpdesk with ticketing and workflow automation. A dedicated FAQ builder feeds custom-trained AI agents that respond like team members, and real-time translation lets one team answer in many languages. Crisp has a long history in live chat outside Shopify and fits merchants running several brands from one workspace. The free plan is a real starting point; Mini costs $45/month, Essentials $95 and Plus $295, with 14-day trials. Its Shopify listing is rated 4.0/5 from 33 reviews, so test the Shopify data sync before moving your whole team over.

#11 Wizybot, AI chatbot plus CRM helpdesk

Wizybot combines an AI chatbot with a CRM-style helpdesk that unifies every chat, including WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and email, in one inbox. Its AI agents handle orders, refunds, FAQs and product recommendations in your brand's tone, and the same AI can reply to comments on social media, which is unusual in this category. The free plan includes 30 AI messages and 30 AI comments, enough to judge answer quality; Basic costs $69.99/month for 1,250 AI messages and 2,500 comments, and a Personalized plan starts at $150/month. Rated 4.6/5 from 167 reviews, Wizybot suits brands whose conversations happen as much in social DMs and comments as on the website.

#12 Chatty, AI chat and help center on a budget

Chatty gives you an AI chatbot that answers product questions, suggests products and helps with order tracking 24/7, a unified inbox for WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and email, and a customizable help center for self-service. Real-time translation and a mobile app round out the package. Pricing is per AI conversation: the free plan includes 50 a month, Basic costs $19.99/month for 100, Pro $68.99 for 500 and Plus $199 for 1,000, with 7-day trials. Rated 4.9/5 from 1,881 reviews, Chatty is a sensible choice for smaller stores that want AI answers and a help center for under $20 a month.

Which chatbot app is right for your store

  • Customers on WhatsApp, or international sales. Kanal, whose AI answers in the customer's language on the channel they already use.
  • Starting for free. Shopify Inbox.
  • Web chat and AI in one widget. Tidio or Chatty, depending on budget.
  • A support team with SLAs. Gorgias or Richpanel.
  • Proactive selling. Rep AI or Manifest AI.
  • Small team, many channels, tight budget. Willdesk, Chatway, Wizybot or Crisp.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best chatbot app for Shopify?

Tidio is the best all-round chatbot app for Shopify: web chat and its Lyro AI agent in one tool, with capped AI usage and a free plan to start. For stores whose customers are on WhatsApp, Kanal is the strongest pick, with AI answers 24/7 in 96+ languages, trained on your store, and handoff to a human in a shared inbox, from $89/month (the AI agent is not part of the entry plan). Gorgias and Richpanel lead for support teams that need a helpdesk, and Shopify Inbox is the best free option.

Can a Shopify chatbot answer questions about specific orders?

Yes, if it is connected to your Shopify order data. Kanal, Tidio, Gorgias, Richpanel, Willdesk and Chatty look up order status from the conversation, and several can send tracking links, cancel an order or start a return. Generic ChatGPT widgets that only read your website pages cannot.

Which Shopify chatbot works on WhatsApp?

Kanal is built for WhatsApp specifically, on the WhatsApp Business API with a Shopify-synced inbox. Tidio, Willdesk, Chatway, Crisp, Wizybot and Chatty include WhatsApp as one channel among several. Our best WhatsApp apps for Shopify round-up compares WhatsApp-only tools.

How much does a Shopify chatbot cost?

Free plans exist (Shopify Inbox, Tidio, Chatty, Willdesk, Chatway), limited by conversations or visitors. Paid AI plans run from about $20 to $100 a month for small stores; helpdesks with autonomous AI agents cost more as volume grows. Kanal starts at $89/month with WhatsApp campaigns and cart recovery included.

Our recommendation

In 2026 the question is less whether to add a chatbot than where it should live. For web chat, Tidio is the safest all-rounder and Shopify Inbox the best free start. If your customers message you on WhatsApp, or you sell internationally, start with Kanal: the AI answers in the customer's language, sells from your real catalog and hands off cleanly, and the same platform runs WhatsApp campaigns and cart recovery. Support-heavy brands should look at Gorgias or Richpanel. Whichever you choose, connect your order data and set handoff rules on day one.
