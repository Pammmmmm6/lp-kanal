Selling clothes online raises questions that most Shopify stores never face. Shoppers cannot try a piece on, so before they buy they want a size they can trust and photos from real customers, and after they buy they send back what does not fit. Each product comes in several sizes and colors, collections change every season, and a sold-out drop leaves demand with nowhere to go. No single app covers all of that, so the practical answer is a stack: one app per need.

What to look for in a clothing store app

A fashion catalog puts specific demands on every app in the stack. Check these points before you install.

  • Sizes and colors. Every product comes in several variants. Look for filters by size and color, a swatch for each color on collection pages, and exchanges for another size.
  • Customer photos. Shoppers want to see the piece on a real person. Look for photo and video reviews, requested after delivery, once the customer has tried the item on.
  • Fit before and after the order. A size chart helps before the purchase. After it, the returns app should make an exchange as easy as a refund. Set the rules first: our guide to writing a Shopify return policy covers the return window, return shipping cost and final sale items.
  • Drops and sell-outs. Limited runs sell out. Waitlists, restock alerts and pre-orders keep that demand, and WhatsApp reaches customers the moment a drop goes live.
  • Pricing that follows your volume. Many apps charge by orders, returns, GMV, bundle sales or wishlist actions. Price your busiest month, not an average one.
  • Overlap between apps. Boost lists pre-order and back in stock on its entry plan, Notify Me! adds a guest wishlist, Swym sends restock and price drop alerts, and Color Swatch King links a size chart. Check what your apps already do before you add another.

#1 Kiwi Size Chart & Recommender, size charts and fit recommendations

Need covered: size guidance and fit.

Kiwi Size Chart & Recommender, by Staytuned, adds customizable size charts and AI-powered size recommendations to your product pages, with more confident purchases and fewer returns as the stated goal. You can import size charts from images, product descriptions or a CSV file, start from 22 pre-built templates, and combine several size tables, images, videos and tabs in one chart. Size guides can apply per product or per collection and attach automatically to new products, and the sizing unit adapts to the customer's location. The free plan publishes up to 3 size charts, with a Kiwi watermark. Premium costs $7.99/month (10 charts, Google Analytics integration, no watermark), Plus $14.99/month (20 charts, up to 10 size recommenders and their performance analytics) and Ultimate $26.99/month (unlimited charts and recommenders, charts translated into 22 languages). Plus and Ultimate add charges based on order volume, and paid plans include a 14-day free trial. Rated 4.7/5 from 1,214 reviews, Kiwi is worth a look if shoppers often ask which size to take.

#2 Judge.me Product Reviews, unlimited photo and video reviews

Need covered: photo and video reviews.

Judge.me Product Reviews collects product and store reviews with photos and videos, and its Forever Free plan keeps both unlimited. Review requests go out automatically after fulfillment or delivery, by email, SMS or push, and reviews appear in widgets, star rating badges, testimonial sliders and video carousels. For a clothing store, customer photos show how a piece looks when worn. The free plan also covers Google reviews sync and rich snippets, trust badges, sync with the Shop App, Etsy and Amazon, and a reviews importer. Awesome costs $15/month, with a 15-day free trial, and adds AI replies, summaries and translations, 16 widgets including Q&A and a reviews page, auto-reminders, coupons and referrals, Google Shopping, Meta and TikTok Shop sync, and 130+ integrations such as Klaviyo and Gorgias. Rated 5/5 from 47,616 reviews, Built for Shopify and a 2025 Build Award winner, Judge.me is worth a look if you want photo reviews at a low fixed price. Kanal's WhatsApp review requests sync with Judge.me.

#3 Loox, visual reviews with AI sorting and referrals

Need covered: photo and video reviews.

Loox is built around visual reviews. It collects photo and video reviews automatically with emails, QR codes and discounts, and shows them in on-brand, mobile-optimized widgets. Its AI features sort the best visual reviews first, highlight key quotes, summarize and translate reviews and draft replies. Reviews sync to and from the Shop App and to Meta Shops, Google Shopping and TikTok Shop, Loox Studio turns reviews into social posts and stories, and the app rewards customer referrals. The free Beginner plan covers up to 500 orders, with review request emails and reminders, discounts for photo reviews, 17+ widgets and Google rich snippets. Convert costs $49.99/month for 300 orders, plus $50 per additional 300 orders, and adds video reviews, the AI features, reviews on product bundles and syndication to Google, Meta and TikTok. Unlimited costs $299.99/month, and paid plans include a 14-day free trial. Rated 4.9/5 from 9,700 reviews and Built for Shopify, Loox is worth a look if photo reviews are central to how you sell. Need covered: returns and exchanges.

AfterShip Returns & Exchanges automates the return process and aims to convert refunds into exchanges or store credit, with incentivized and personalized exchange offers and fraud checks. Customers use a branded returns page, while rules and automation handle approvals, refunds and updates in the back end. Built-in analytics surface the product and listing issues behind returns. It adds discounted labels and in-store returns through Shopify POS, and works with Canada Post, FedEx, UPS, USPS and Gorgias. The Return Care plan is listed as free: returns are funded by shoppers, labels are handled by AfterShip and a revenue share applies. Essentials costs $19/month ($1 for the first month) with 20 returns a month and $0.50 per extra return. Premium costs $119/month with 100 returns, $1 per extra return, advanced workflows, AI-powered exchanges, fraud prevention and a warranty portal. Both include a 7-day free trial. Rated 4.7/5 from 1,542 reviews and Built for Shopify, AfterShip Returns is worth a look if you want exchanges and store credit at a low entry price.

Need covered: returns and exchanges.

Loop Returns & Exchanges automates what happens after checkout: returns, exchanges, order editing, tracking and fraud prevention. The most useful Shopify apps answer the questions shoppers ask before and after they buy.
