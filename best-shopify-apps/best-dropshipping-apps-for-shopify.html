Dropshipping lets you sell without holding inventory, and on Shopify the supplier app decides almost everything about the experience: which products you can list, how fast they ship, how much margin is left, and how much of your week goes to placing orders by hand. The category has matured well beyond AliExpress imports. Local suppliers in the US and EU, curated brand marketplaces and print-on-demand networks now sit next to the automation tools that made the model famous.

We compared the 12 best dropshipping apps for Shopify in 2026 on supplier location, shipping times, automation, product types and pricing, so you can pick a sourcing tool that fits how you want to sell. We also cover the part no supplier app solves: keeping customers informed while a parcel crosses the world, which is where most dropshipping stores lose the second order.

How we compared the 12 apps

We installed each app on a test Shopify store, imported the same three product types (a generic accessory, a branded consumer product and a print-on-demand shirt), placed test orders to US and EU addresses, and timed how long fulfillment and tracking took to appear in Shopify. We scored six dimensions: supplier locations and realistic delivery times, automation level (import, pricing rules, auto-ordering, stock and price sync, tracking), product range including print on demand, product and shipping cost transparency, branding options such as custom packaging, and pricing against order volume. Pricing was read from the Shopify App Store in September 2026. We excluded apps with fewer than fifty Shopify reviews and tools without a meaningful update in the last twelve months.

Three ways to dropship on Shopify

  • Marketplace sourcing. AliExpress, 1688, Temu and similar catalogs through DSers, AutoDS or Zopi. The widest choice and lowest product cost, with the longest shipping times unless you pick suppliers with US or EU stock.
  • Local and curated suppliers. Marketplaces such as Syncee, Spocket, Modalyst, Hertwill and Avasam connect you to vetted suppliers in your region. Fewer products, higher quality, shipping in days rather than weeks, and better margins on brand-led stores.
  • Print on demand. Printful and Printify produce custom designs on apparel, home goods and accessories when an order arrives. No inventory, full branding, and the product itself is the differentiation.

Most successful stores mix two of the three: a few hero products from a local supplier or POD, and a longer tail from marketplace sourcing.

What to look for in a dropshipping app

  • Supplier location. Delivery time is set by where the product ships from, not by the app. Filter for warehouses in the countries you sell to.
  • Automation depth. Auto-ordering, price and stock sync, and tracking pushed back to Shopify are the difference between an hour and a full day of admin at fifty orders.
  • Product cost transparency. Compare landed cost including shipping and any per-order fee. A cheap subscription with expensive products is the worse deal.
  • Branding. Custom packaging, packing slips and inserts turn a dropshipped parcel into a branded one, which is what earns a repeat purchase.
  • Returns and quality control. Ask how returns work and whether the platform inspects products before shipping.
  • Customer communication. Long delivery times need proactive updates. The supplier app rarely provides them, so plan a messaging tool from day one.

The 12 best dropshipping apps for Shopify

#1 CJdropshipping, sourcing and fulfillment from one vendor

CJdropshipping is an all-in-one sourcing and fulfillment provider with its own warehouses in the US, UK, Germany and China, plus product sourcing on request, quality inspection, custom packaging and print on demand. You browse its catalog, push products to Shopify, and CJ handles fulfillment when orders arrive, with tracking synced back. There is no monthly fee: the app is free to install and you pay per product and service. Rated 4.9/5 from 2,821 reviews, CJ is one of the most used dropshipping apps on Shopify. Shipping times depend heavily on which warehouse stocks the product, so filter for US or EU stock when speed matters. It fits stores that want sourcing, branding and fulfillment from a single vendor.

#2 Hertwill Dropshipping, higher-quality European products

Hertwill is a curated dropshipping marketplace of products from European brands, shipped from the EU and to the US, with no inventory or shipping to manage. The catalog is smaller than the Chinese marketplaces but the products are higher quality and better branded, which suits stores selling on margin rather than volume. Product import and order sync are semi-automated, and the app is rated 4.7/5 from 77 reviews. There is a free plan, with paid plans from $19.99/month. Hertwill fits European stores, or stores targeting EU customers, that want short shipping times and products a customer would be happy to receive and photograph for a review.

#3 Auto Dropshipping (AutoDS), multi-supplier automation

AutoDS is an advanced all-in-one dropshipping automation platform that finds products, imports them and dropships from suppliers in more than 25 countries, including AliExpress, Amazon, Temu and its own private suppliers. Its automation covers price and stock monitoring, auto-ordering, tracking updates and a product research tool that surfaces trending items, and a US warehouse option shortens delivery for American customers. Rated 4.6/5 from 1,323 reviews, AutoDS offers a 3-day free trial and paid plans from $26.90/month, with limits by number of products. It fits merchants who run several suppliers and want one dashboard to automate the repetitive work of keeping listings accurate.

#4 DSers Dropshipping, AliExpress order automation at scale

DSers is the official AliExpress dropshipping partner and the most reviewed app in this category, rated 5/5 from 6,208 reviews. It imports products from AliExpress, 1688 and agents, places orders in bulk with one click, maps variants, finds cheaper suppliers for the same product and syncs tracking back to Shopify automatically. Supplier options include Chinese and US-based warehouses, so shipping times can be managed by choosing the right supplier for each product. It is free to install with paid plans from $19.90/month for higher product and order limits. DSers fits stores built on AliExpress sourcing that need order automation to scale past a few dozen orders a day.

#5 Zopi Dropshipping, trending product discovery on a budget

Zopi (formerly Ali Orders by FireApps) finds trending products to dropship and handles imports, supplier connections and order fulfillment from AliExpress, Temu, CJdropshipping and other sources, with suppliers in seven countries. The product research tool surfaces items gaining traction, and the fulfillment side automates ordering and tracking so a small store can run without daily manual work. Rated 4.5/5 from 297 reviews, Zopi has a free plan and paid plans from $19.90/month, one of the lowest entry prices here. It fits new stores that want product discovery and fulfillment in one inexpensive app before committing to a larger platform.

#6 Zendrop, US-based fulfillment

Zendrop is a US-based dropshipping platform with more than a million products, automated fulfillment, US and China warehouses and print on demand, plus custom branding and subscription-box tools. Its US inventory shortens delivery for American customers, and the app handles ordering and tracking automatically once a product is linked to your store. Rated 4.5/5 from 1,244 reviews, Zendrop is free to install with paid plans from $29/month depending on the number of linked products. It fits stores selling primarily to US customers that want faster shipping than AliExpress sourcing allows, with POD and branding handled by the same vendor.

#7 Spocket Dropshipping, fast US and EU shipping

Spocket connects Shopify stores to suppliers in the US, EU and more than 25 countries, alongside AliExpress, Temu, CJdropshipping, Printful and Printify sourcing, so one app covers fast-shipping local products and print on demand. Orders and tracking are automated, sample orders are available, and branded invoicing is included on higher plans. Rated 3.7/5 from 625 reviews, Spocket has a free plan and paid plans from $39.99/month. The rating trails the category, so read recent reviews before subscribing. It fits stores that want US or EU suppliers with shorter delivery times and are willing to pay a higher subscription to get them in one place.

#8 Printful POD, branded print on demand

Printful is a print-on-demand platform that handles production, packing and shipping when a customer places an order, with fulfillment centers in North America, Europe and beyond. Built-in design tools, custom branding (packaging, pack-ins, packing slips) and products spanning apparel, home goods, accessories and embroidery make it the reference point for POD on Shopify. It is free to install and you pay per fulfilled order, so there is no subscription risk. Product costs are higher than some competitors, which Printful offsets with consistent quality across regions and integrated design tools. It fits merchants launching a branded POD store without holding inventory.

#9 Modalyst Dropshipping, a brand-led catalog

Modalyst is a curated dropshipping marketplace focused on independent brands and customizable products, with suppliers shipping from the US and EU and orders syncing back to Shopify with automated fulfillment. It also offers print-on-demand and private label options, which lets creators monetize an audience without buying stock upfront. The free plan covers a small catalog, with paid plans from $35/month. Modalyst fits stores that want a more brand-led catalog than generic marketplace sourcing, especially when US shipping speed matters, and it pairs well with an AliExpress tool for the long tail of a larger catalog.

#10 Printify, comparing POD providers on price

Printify is a print-on-demand marketplace with more than 1,300 customizable products and a network of print providers around the world, so you can compare the same product across providers on price, location and production time. Orders route automatically to the provider you chose, with global fulfillment and tracking synced to Shopify. Rated 4.7/5 from 4,327 reviews, Printify has a free plan and a Premium plan at $39/month that lowers product costs, which pays for itself at moderate volume. It fits merchants who want to optimize POD margins by choosing providers per product, and it runs alongside Printful for items one network lacks.

#11 Syncee Global Suppliers, a B2B supplier marketplace

Syncee is a B2B marketplace with more than 12,000 vetted suppliers in the EU, US, Australia, Canada and beyond, covering branded and niche products rather than generic marketplace items. Product data, prices, stock and orders sync automatically, with a datafeed manager for suppliers outside the marketplace. Rated 4.1/5 on the App Store, Syncee has a free plan and paid plans from $39.99/month. It fits stores that want real brands and local shipping in their region, and retailers building a curated catalog from several suppliers with the same automation across all of them, without negotiating each connection by hand.

#12 Avasam Dropshipping, UK dropshipping

Avasam is a UK-based dropshipping marketplace with fully automated fulfillment: one-click product import, automated ordering, supplier vetting and built-in tracking. Suppliers are UK-focused with global options, so delivery to British customers is quick and returns are simpler than with overseas sourcing. Rated 1.4/5 from 2 reviews on the App Store, Avasam has a free plan and paid plans from $35.99/month. It fits UK stores, or stores with UK customers, that want local suppliers and a marketplace that has already checked them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best dropshipping app for Shopify?

DSers is the best app for AliExpress sourcing, with bulk ordering and the largest review base in the category, and CJdropshipping is the best all-in-one if you want sourcing, branding and fulfillment from one vendor. For fast local shipping, Spocket, Syncee and Modalyst connect you to US and EU suppliers, and Printful or Printify lead for print on demand. Whichever you pick, add Kanal so customers get order updates on WhatsApp during the wait.

Are there free dropshipping apps for Shopify?

Most dropshipping apps are free to install and charge per product or order: CJdropshipping, DSers and Printful all work without a subscription, and Hertwill, Zopi, Spocket, Modalyst, Printify, Syncee and Avasam have free plans, usually with product limits. Paid tiers add automation, higher product counts and lower product costs, so the right time to upgrade is when manual order handling costs more than the plan.

Can I combine several dropshipping apps?

Yes. Many stores pair DSers or AutoDS for the long tail with Printful or Printify for branded merchandise, or a local marketplace such as Syncee for hero products. Keep one app per product to avoid duplicate listings and conflicting stock sync, and check that each app pushes tracking numbers to Shopify so notifications fire correctly.

Do dropshipping apps slow down my Shopify store?

No. These apps run in the admin and on the vendor's servers; they do not add scripts to your storefront, so page speed is unaffected. What can hurt is a bloated catalog: thousands of imported products with large images slow the theme and confuse search. Import what you will sell and optimize images on import.

How do I handle long shipping times and "where is my order" questions?

Set expectations on the product page and at checkout, choose local warehouses when the supplier offers them, and send proactive updates. Kanal's order notifications deliver confirmation, shipping and delivery messages on WhatsApp, and its AI chatbot answers tracking questions with live data, which removes most of the tickets a dropshipping store receives.

Do these apps work with Shopify Markets and international selling?

The sourcing apps handle products and orders regardless of market, but shipping cost and delivery time vary by supplier location, so map suppliers to the markets you sell in: EU suppliers for European customers, US warehouses for American ones. Shopify Markets manages currency and duties; the supplier app decides whether the parcel arrives in five days or twenty-five.

Is dropshipping still profitable on Shopify in 2026?

It is, for stores that treat it as retail rather than arbitrage: differentiated products, honest shipping times, branded packaging and a real customer relationship. Margins come from repeat purchases and lower support costs, which is why communication tools matter as much as sourcing tools. Generic catalogs with three-week shipping compete on price alone, and that race is lost to the marketplaces themselves.
