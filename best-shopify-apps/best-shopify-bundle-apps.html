Understanding bundle types

Before choosing an app, it helps to know the four main bundle strategies used on Shopify.

Fixed bundles group specific products together at a set discount. A skincare brand selling a cleanser, toner, and moisturizer as a "Complete Routine" kit is a classic example. Fixed bundles work best when products have a natural relationship and when you want tight control over what ships together.

Mix-and-match bundles let shoppers pick from a selection to build their own bundle. Think "choose any 3 teas" or "build your own snack box." These suit stores with large catalogs and repeat buyers who want variety.

Volume discounts, also called quantity breaks, reward customers for buying multiples of the same item. "Buy 2 get 10% off, buy 3 get 20% off" is the standard pattern. This approach suits consumable products and basics where customers already plan to restock.

BOGO, buy one get one, offers create urgency and suit clearance, seasonal promotions, or introductory offers on new products. Pair bundles with post-purchase upsell flows to recommend add-ons after checkout.

How to choose a Shopify bundle app

The bundle category is full of apps that look similar in screenshots. These points decide which one fits your store.

  • Start with Shopify's own app. If you only need fixed bundles and multipacks, the free Shopify Bundles app may be enough (see the next section). Move to a third-party app for mix-and-match, quantity breaks, BOGO or A/B tests.
  • Bundle types and discounts. Confirm the listing names the offers you plan to run, and that it handles percentage, fixed-amount and fixed-price discounts.
  • Inventory and fulfillment. Check how the app tracks the stock of each component. Shopify Bundles updates inventory in real time, and Simple Bundles lists inventory sync across 3PL, WMS and ERP systems.
  • Theme and page builders. A bundle widget should fit your product and cart pages. Several listings name the page builders and cart drawer apps they work with, such as PageFly and GemPages. Test on your live theme before committing.
  • Analytics and testing. Look for reporting on the revenue each bundle adds, and for A/B testing if you want to compare discount levels: Rapi, Wide Bundles, Kaching Bundles, UFE and Rebolt list it.
  • Pricing model. Many plans are capped by the revenue the app generates (Rapi, Wide Bundles, Kaching Bundles, Fast Bundle), by orders (UFE, Frequently Bought Together, Simple Bundles' free plan) or by your Shopify plan (Simple Bundles). Price a strong month, not an average one. Our Shopify pricing guide explains the plans themselves.

Does Shopify have a free bundle app?

Yes. Shopify Bundles is, in the words of Shopify's Help Center (read October 8, 2026), "a free first-party bundles app" for fixed product bundles and multipacks, created from the Shopify admin and available on all Shopify plans. Customers can choose variants such as size or color for the products in the bundle, and inventory updates in real time. Its App Store listing shows a rating of 2.9/5 from 568 reviews.

The Help Center also lists its limits: up to 30 components in a fixed bundle, a maximum of 3 options and 100 variants per bundle, inventory tracked by overall stock rather than by location, and a bundle price that does not update when the price of a component changes. Its listing names two bundle types, fixed bundles and multipacks. For mix-and-match, quantity breaks or A/B tests, see the twelve apps below.

Which bundle type works best for your business

Choosing the right bundle strategy depends on your catalog and customer behavior.

Single-product or hero-product brands usually start with volume discounts. If you sell one flagship item, quantity breaks encourage customers to stock up. Wide Bundles, Kaching Bundles and Bundler's free plan all list quantity breaks.

Catalog-heavy stores with diverse products can explore mix-and-match bundles. Rapi, Fast Bundle, Rebolt and Bundler's Premium plan let customers build their own combination from your catalog.

Gift-oriented or seasonal businesses often prefer fixed bundles that tell a story. A curated "Summer Essentials" or "New Parent Kit" removes the decision burden. Shopify Bundles, Bold Bundles and Simple Bundles handle fixed bundles and kits.

Stores focused on cross-selling can look at Frequently Bought Together or UFE Cross Sell & Upsell, which show recommended products next to the one being viewed.

Getting bundles seen: promotion matters as much as setup

Building a great bundle is only half the job. If shoppers never notice it, it cannot lift your average order value. The same channels that promote a bundle also bring back the shoppers who leave it in the cart, which is why we pair this guide with our best Shopify abandoned cart apps round-up. On-site placement on product and cart pages does most of the work, but promotion helps too. Email campaigns announcing a new bundle, and messaging channels such as WhatsApp where customers can reply directly, can bring repeat buyers straight to a curated offer. Note that messaging tools like Kanal do not build bundles themselves. They are a promotion layer you would pair with one of the bundle apps above, not a replacement for it. Choose your bundle app first, then decide how you will draw attention to your offers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best bundle app for Shopify?

Fast Bundle is #1 in our ranking: it builds mix-and-match and fixed bundles, BOGO, free gifts and AI-powered frequently bought together, is rated 5/5 from 3,520 reviews, and paid plans start at $19/month. Rapi covers bundles, mix and match, volume discounts and post-purchase upsells with A/B testing, has a free plan for up to $100 a month of revenue generated, and paid plans start at $15/month. Bundler includes volume discounts on a free plan with no revenue limit, Simple Bundles is built around inventory and fulfillment, and Shopify's own Bundles app covers fixed bundles and multipacks at no cost. All figures were read on the App Store on October 8, 2026.

Does Shopify have a free bundle app?

Yes. Shopify Bundles is a free app made by Shopify, available on all Shopify plans, for fixed bundles and multipacks created from the admin. According to Shopify's Help Center (read October 8, 2026), a fixed bundle holds up to 30 components, with a maximum of 3 options and 100 variants per bundle, and the bundle price does not update when a component's price changes. Among third-party apps, Bundler, Rapi, UFE, Simple Bundles and Frequently Bought Together have free plans with their own limits.

What are the best alternatives to Kaching Bundles on Shopify?

Kaching Bundles focuses on bundle deals and quantity breaks, from $14.99/month for up to $1,000 of additional revenue. For the same kind of offer, look at Wide Bundles (from $14.99/month, 100+ design options and A/B testing), Rapi (free plan, then $15/month) and Bundler, whose free plan includes volume discounts and quantity breaks with no revenue limit. For mix-and-match bundles, Fast Bundle and Rebolt are worth a look, and Simple Bundles if inventory sync with a 3PL matters most.

Do bundle apps slow down my Shopify store?

Any app that adds a widget to product pages adds scripts to load, so measure rather than assume. Check your page speed before and after installing the app, on a product page with a bundle and on mobile. Running one app that covers several jobs can mean fewer scripts than several separate apps, which is the approach Vitals describes on its listing. Remove the widgets you do not use.

Can I use multiple bundle apps at the same time?

Technically yes, but it is rarely a good idea. Overlapping bundle widgets can confuse customers and create conflicts in discount logic. Pick one app for building bundles and promote those offers through your existing email and messaging channels.

Do bundles work with Shopify's native discount system?

With Shopify Bundles, yes: Shopify's Help Center says product bundles support discount codes and automatic discounts, as long as the discount includes the parent bundle product. Third-party apps often apply their own discount logic, so bundle discounts and store-wide sales can stack in unintended ways. Check your app's settings to control how discounts combine, and test before launching a bundle alongside a site-wide promotion.

What is a realistic AOV increase from bundling?

There is no reliable public benchmark: the lift depends on your niche, your products, and execution quality. Stores selling complementary products, like a camera shop bundling a body, lens, and bag, have the most to gain. Measure it on your own store: show the bundle to only part of your visitors with an A/B test, as Rapi or Kaching Bundles allow, and compare the average order value of the two groups over the same period. Our average order value guide explains the calculation.

Do bundle discounts hurt my margins?

Only if you design them poorly. On a multi-unit sale, a well-calibrated bundle discount can protect gross margin because packaging and shipping are shared, and customer acquisition cost is spread across more revenue. Run the numbers before launch: the discount should stay below the margin on the extra units sold plus the packaging and shipping savings. The risk shows up when you bundle low-margin items just to lift order count: that lifts revenue without lifting profit. Pick bundle components with healthy margins, and use your app's analytics to confirm each bundle adds profit.

How do bundle apps work with Shopify Markets?

It depends on the app, so check the listing. UFE Cross Sell & Upsell states that it is fully compatible with Shopify Markets, and Bundler mentions localized bundle offers in multiple languages and multicurrency converter apps. Whatever you choose, test each bundle in every market and currency before launch, since discounts and rounded prices can display differently from one market to the next.

Bundle vs upsell, which converts better?

They work at different moments. A bundle widget on a product or cart page reframes the order while it is still being shaped. Upsells, especially post-purchase upsells, come after checkout, when payment is done and the buyer has shown clear intent. Many Shopify stores layer both: a bundle on the product page, and a one-click post-purchase upsell for add-ons. Track the effect on your conversion rate as well as on order value.

Should I rely on bundles for my BFCM strategy?

Bundles suit Black Friday and Cyber Monday because shoppers are looking for value-driven offers, but they should not be your only lever. Pair a flagship bundle with a clear promotion calendar and WhatsApp campaigns or email broadcasts so existing customers see the offer the moment it goes live.
