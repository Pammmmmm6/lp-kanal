How to add reviews to a Shopify store

  1. Choose a review app. Shopify's own Product Reviews app is no longer an option: its App Store listing says the app is not currently available (read October 7, 2026). The 10 apps below are.
  2. Install it and bring in existing reviews. Judge.me, Loox and Rivyo list importers for reviews collected in other apps or on marketplaces.
  3. Add the review widget to your product page. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store, click Edit theme, open the product page, click Add block and pick the app's block under Apps, then save. Shopify's Help Center describes how app blocks work.
  4. Turn on automatic review requests and set the delay after delivery (see the next section).
  5. If you sell on Shop, connect it. Shop collects its own product reviews after delivery, and you choose when the request goes out, from 1 to 180 days after delivery, under Sales channels, Shop, Settings (Shopify Help Center). Shopify lists five partner apps that sync with Shop reviews: Judge.me, Loox, Okendo, Stamped.io and Yotpo.

Where and when to ask for a review

Timing beats template. Ask after the product has been used, not when it ships: a few days after delivery for most categories, two to three weeks for supplements and skincare. Ask on the channel the customer opens. Email requests are the default and the easiest to ignore. SMS lifts response for mobile-heavy audiences. On WhatsApp, the customer replies right in the chat, which matters most in markets where the app is the main messaging app. Ask once, remind once, and make the answer easy on a phone: a three-tap form, or a reply in the chat. Everything else, from widgets to syndication, only matters once the reviews exist.

What to look for in a Shopify review app

  • Collection channels. Email is table stakes. SMS, WhatsApp and an on-site popup each add reviews the others miss.
  • Formats. Photo and video reviews show the product in real use. Attribute ratings such as fit or quality give shoppers structured answers.
  • Display and speed. Widgets load on every product page. Prefer apps built on theme app extensions with deferred scripts.
  • Syndication. Rich snippets for Google search, Google Shopping ratings, and sync to Meta, TikTok Shop, the Shop App or Trustpilot, depending on where you sell.
  • Moderation and replies. You should be able to reply publicly, hide reviews that break your rules and route negative feedback to support.
  • Incentives. Coupons for any review, never only for positive ones, lift response without breaking platform rules.
  • Pricing model. Most apps price by monthly orders or review invites. Check the cap against your volume during peak months.

The 10 best Shopify review apps

#1 Judge.me Review, unlimited reviews on a free plan

Judge.me is the most reviewed app in this comparison, rated 5/5 from 48,126 reviews, and it carries the Built for Shopify badge. Requests go out automatically by email, SMS and push after fulfillment or delivery. The Forever Free plan includes unlimited product and store reviews, unlimited photo and video reviews, a review widget, carousels, Google reviews sync with rich snippets, and sync with the Shop App, Etsy and Amazon. The Awesome plan costs $15/month, with a 15-day free trial. It adds AI replies, summaries and translations, 16 widgets including Q&A and a reviews page, auto-reminders, coupons and referrals, Google Shopping, and Meta and TikTok Shop sync. Judge.me also imports reviews from Yotpo, Loox, Amazon and Etsy, which eases a migration.

#2 Loox Review, visual reviews with referrals

Loox focuses on photo and video reviews. It collects them automatically with emails, QR codes and discounts, and shows them in on-brand widgets built to load fast on mobile. AI features sort the best visual reviews first, highlight key quotes, and translate and reply to reviews. Reviews sync to and from the Shop App, and to Meta Shops, Google Shopping and TikTok Shop. Loox Studio turns them into social posts and stories. Rated 4.9/5 from 9,771 reviews, Loox says 100,000 ecommerce businesses use it. The Beginner plan is free up to 500 orders, with review request emails, discounts for photo reviews and 17+ widgets. Convert costs $49.99/month for 300 orders, plus $50 per additional 300 orders, and adds video reviews, AI features, referrals and syndication. Unlimited costs $299.99/month. Both paid plans come with a 14-day free trial.

#3 Reviews.io, product and company reviews

REVIEWS.io collects product reviews, company reviews and photo or video UGC. Requests go out by automated email and SMS, or through Klaviyo and Shopify Flow. Reviews display in 15+ widgets, and AI-powered analytics summarize what customers say. The app syndicates reviews to TikTok Shop and the Shop App, and it is part of the Clearer.io suite. Rated 4.7/5 from 768 reviews, it has a free plan with 25 review invites a month. Launch costs $19/month for 50 invites and adds Google Seller Ratings and rich snippets. Launch Plus costs $45/month for 500 invites, with surveys, NPS, Q&A and AI review summaries. Both paid plans offer a 14-day free trial, and the listing notes that Reviews.io may bill some charges outside your Shopify invoice.

#4 Rivyo Review, review import and loyalty add-ons

Rivyo collects photo, video and star reviews through automated post-purchase emails. It imports reviews from Judge.me, Yotpo, Loox, Okendo, Amazon, AliExpress and Etsy. Widgets cover carousels, testimonials, store reviews, Q&A and pop-ups, with Google rich snippets. Loyalty and referral programs are add-ons at $9/month each. Rated 4.9/5 from 936 reviews, Rivyo is free for up to 100 orders a month. Starter costs $9/month for 500 orders. Business costs $14/month for 1,000 orders, with coupon rewards and QR code collection. Enterprise costs $19/month, with unlimited orders, unlimited video reviews and AI review autopilot. Each paid plan has a 7-day free trial. Imported reviews should be disclosed as such.

#5 Stamped Review, reviews plus loyalty

Stamped combines product reviews, photos, videos and Q&A with a loyalty program built on points, rewards, VIP tiers and referrals. Review requests and loyalty campaigns run automatically by email and SMS. Google Shopping and rich snippets support SEO, and the app works with Klaviyo, Gorgias, Recharge, Facebook and Instagram. Rated 4.7/5 from 3,752 reviews, Stamped has no free plan on its listing. The Reviews plan starts at $23/month for up to 200 orders a month, then $99/month for 1,000 orders and $199/month for 5,000 orders. The Loyalty plan starts at $299/month for 5,000 orders.

#6 Yotpo Reviews, reviews connected to Yotpo Loyalty

Yotpo collects product and site reviews on autopilot, with in-mail review requests, email templates and AI-powered prompts. Smart Filters, carousels and AI summaries shape the display. Google Seller Ratings and Google Shoppable Ads extend reviews to search, and reviews sync to and from platforms such as TikTok and Walmart. Yotpo Reviews also connects with Yotpo Loyalty. Rated 4.8/5 from 4,625 reviews, Yotpo has a free plan for up to 50 monthly orders with automatic review requests. Starter costs $15/month and adds photo and video collection, rich snippets and Google Shopping Ads. Pro costs $119/month with Google Seller Rating, custom questions and AI review summaries. Prices change with order volume. Our Yotpo pricing guide explains how the plans move with your order tier.

Junip includes unlimited review requests on every plan, with mobile-first forms designed to raise the submission rate and fast, on-brand widgets. Frequently asked questions

What is the best Shopify review app?

Judge.me, #1 in our ranking, is the Shopify review app we recommend to most stores: it is the most reviewed app in this comparison (48,126 reviews, rated 5/5), and its free plan includes unlimited product, photo and video reviews with Google reviews sync. Loox is built around photo and video reviews, and the other apps on this list differ mainly on free plan limits, syndication and add-ons such as loyalty or Q&A. To collect more reviews, look at the request channel: Kanal asks on WhatsApp after delivery and syncs the replies with Judge.me, Loox or Shopify native reviews, and Reviewz.ai sends invitations to Google and Trustpilot.

How do I add reviews to my Shopify store?

Install a review app from the Shopify App Store, since Shopify's own Product Reviews app is no longer available there. Then open the theme editor (Online Store, Edit theme), go to your product page, click Add block, pick the app's review block under Apps and save. Finally, turn on automatic review requests in the app. Judge.me, #1 here, includes the widget and the requests on its free plan.

Which Shopify review apps work with Google?

All ten listings mention Google (read October 7, 2026). Judge.me lists Google reviews sync and rich snippets on its free plan, and Google Shopping on Awesome. Loox, Rivyo and Okendo list Google rich snippets on their free plans. Reviews.io and Yotpo list Google Seller Ratings on paid plans, Stamped and Junip list Google Shopping, Fera syncs reviews from Google, and Trustpilot names Google among its integrations.

Does Reviews.io work with Shopify?

Yes. REVIEWS.io has its own Shopify App Store listing, rated 4.7/5 from 768 reviews on October 7, 2026. It collects product and company reviews by email and SMS, works with Shopify Flow and Klaviyo, and syndicates reviews to the Shop app and TikTok Shop. The free plan includes 25 review invites a month, Launch costs $19/month and Launch Plus $45/month.

Are there free review apps for Shopify?

Yes. Judge.me's Forever Free plan includes unlimited product and store reviews, and Junip's free plan includes unlimited review request emails. Loox is free up to 500 orders, Rivyo up to 100 orders a month, and Yotpo and Okendo up to 50 orders a month. Reviews.io includes 25 review invites a month and Trustpilot 50 invitations. Fera offers a 60-day free trial instead of a free plan, and Stamped starts at $23/month. Check the cap against your order volume in peak months.

Do review apps slow down my Shopify store?

Review widgets load on every product page, so they matter for Core Web Vitals. Loox and Junip describe their widgets as fast on their listings. Test a product page with Lighthouse on a mobile profile before and after install, and use the app's theme app extension rather than pasting code into your theme. Kanal collects reviews on WhatsApp and syncs them to your review app, so the display stays with that app.

Can I use two review apps at the same time?

Yes, when they do different jobs. A common pairing is a product review app for on-site display, such as Judge.me or Loox, plus Kanal to request reviews on WhatsApp and sync them back to that app. Reviewz.ai can add Google and Trustpilot invitations on top. Avoid two apps that both display product reviews: duplicate widgets and split review counts hurt more than they help.

Do review apps support Shopify Markets and multiple languages?

It depends on the app. Judge.me's Awesome plan includes AI translations, Loox's Convert plan adds AI translations of reviews, and Junip lists multi-language display among its features. Most listings show the app interface in English only, so check how request emails and widgets handle each storefront language. Under Shopify Markets, also check whether reviews collected in one market display in another.

Can I offer a discount for leaving a review?

You can offer an incentive for leaving a review, but not for leaving a positive one, and the review should disclose it where the platform requires. Judge.me includes coupons on its Awesome plan, and Loox offers discounts for photo reviews on its free plan. Rivyo adds coupon rewards from $14/month, Junip lists incentives from $29/month, and Fera rewards reviews with discounts, points or cashback. Google and Trustpilot have their own rules on incentives, so keep incentivized reviews on-site and collect off-site reviews without rewards.

How do I get more reviews without buying more traffic?

Ask on a channel customers open. Email requests are easy to ignore. A WhatsApp message sent a few days after delivery lands in the customer's messaging app, and with Kanal's review collection flow the customer answers right in the chat, with text, photos or videos. Reviewz.ai sends WhatsApp, SMS and email invitations for Google and Trustpilot. Then remind once, keep the request short and show existing reviews prominently.
