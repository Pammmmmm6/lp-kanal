Plans and billing

How much does Kanal cost?

Kanal has four plans: Pro at 89€, Scale at 149€, Business at 350€ and Agency from 1,900€ per month. Pay monthly with no commitment, or yearly with 10% off.

Can I try Kanal for free?

Yes. Kanal installs for free from the Shopify App Store with a demo plan, so you can explore it before choosing a plan.

What is the difference between Pro and Scale?

Scale adds 5 users, custom segments, unlimited and advanced automations, the AI agent, Kanal MCP, integrations like Klaviyo and Gorgias, and multi-market.

What does done for you mean on Business?

Our experts set up your flows, keep optimizing them and prepare campaigns on demand, with a dedicated account manager.

Is there a commitment?

Monthly plans have no commitment. Yearly plans run for 12 months and cost 10% less.

How does multi-market work?

From Scale, each extra market is added for 50€ per month on top of your plan.

Which integrations are included?

Klaviyo, Gorgias, Loyoly, Crisp and Recharge are included from Scale, and Splio is coming soon.

Is Kanal an official WhatsApp provider?

Yes. Kanal runs on the official WhatsApp Business API and is an Official Meta Business Partner.

What is the Agency plan?

For agencies and multi-brand groups: WhatsApp strategy driven by our team, creative production, campaign copywriting and consolidated reporting per client.
