    FAQ

    What is Kanal and how does it work?

    Kanal is a WhatsApp marketing platform designed for e-commerce brands. It integrates directly with your Shopify store to automate cart recovery, send targeted campaigns, and provide 24/7 customer support through WhatsApp.

    How does Kanal integrate with Shopify?

    Kanal connects seamlessly with your Shopify store in just a few clicks. Once installed, it automatically syncs your products, customer data, and order information to power personalized WhatsApp campaigns.

    What kind of ROI can I expect?

    Our merchants see an average 12X return on investment. With 96%+ open rates on WhatsApp compared to ~20% for email, your messages actually get seen and acted upon.

    How is WhatsApp marketing different from email?

    WhatsApp delivers 96%+ open rates vs ~20% for email. Messages feel personal and conversational, leading to higher engagement, faster responses, and significantly better conversion rates.
