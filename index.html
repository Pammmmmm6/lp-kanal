    FAQ

    Which apps can Kanal use?

    Anything you can open in a browser or on a computer: CRMs, spreadsheets, email, internal tools. Native integrations speed up the most common ones.

    Can I review an action before it runs?

    Yes. For each agent, choose which actions need your sign-off, such as sending an email, making a payment or deleting data.

    How many agents can I create?

    As many as you need. How many can run at the same time depends on your plan.

    What happens if an agent makes a mistake?

    Every action is logged and most can be undone. Point out the error in the thread: the agent fixes it and adds a note so it won't happen again.

    Where is my data stored?

    In European data centers, encrypted in transit and at rest. Any access you grant an agent can be revoked at any time.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Monthly plans have no commitment, and annual plans are billed upfront at a discount.
