SMS marketing rules to know before you send

SMS is a regulated channel. Four rules to know before your first campaign, with the texts they come from.

Consent comes first

In France, marketing by SMS is allowed only if the person agreed beforehand; the exception covers existing customers and similar products or services. In the United States, the carriers’ association asks senders to obtain the consumer’s opt-in. On Shopify, an SMS sign-up checkbox can be added to the checkout.

Sources: CNIL, CTIA Messaging Principles and Best Practices, Shopify Help Center

A sender people can identify, and a STOP

Each message must let the recipient know who is writing and refuse further messages by a simple means. In practice: your brand name at the start, an opt-out mention at the end.

Sources: CNIL, CTIA Messaging Principles and Best Practices

Sending hours

In the United States, federal rules bar telephone solicitations, text messages included, before 8 a.m. and after 9 p.m. in the recipient’s local time. In France, the operators’ charter allows promotional SMS between 8:00 and 21:30. Check the rules of each country you send to.

Sources: 47 CFR 64.1200, af2m

160 characters per SMS

A standard SMS holds 160 characters in the GSM alphabet. Beyond that, or with characters outside that alphabet, the text is split into several parts. One idea, one link.

Sources: 3GPP TS 23.038, 3GPP TS 23.040

General information, not legal advice. The rules depend on the countries you send to: check them before sending.

Frequently asked questions about SMS marketing

What software do I need to send bulk SMS?

It depends on where your contacts live. For an online store, the simplest route is SMS marketing software connected to the store: it picks up your customers, their orders and their consent, with no file to import. Kanal works this way for Shopify stores: SMS campaigns to your segments, automated texts and sales tracking.

How do I send a bulk SMS to my customers?

In three moves: gather the numbers of customers who agreed to receive your texts, write a short message with your brand name and an opt-out mention, then send it from SMS software. In Kanal, the list is a segment of your store and each customer receives the text individually.

How much does an SMS campaign cost?

The cost of an SMS campaign depends on the destination country and on the number of segments: a text longer than 160 characters counts as several SMS. It comes on top of the software subscription. Book a demo to get an estimate for your volumes and countries.

Can I send business SMS from a computer?

Yes. SMS software runs in the browser: you type the message on your keyboard, choose the recipients and the send goes out without your phone. That is how Kanal is used.

What are the rules for SMS marketing?

They depend on the country. The common base: the recipient agreed beforehand, the message says who is sending it, and it offers a simple way to opt out, such as the STOP keyword. Many countries also restrict sending hours: in the United States, no solicitation before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. in the recipient’s local time.

Can SMS be automated?

Yes. An automated SMS goes out when an event happens in the store: abandoned cart, order shipped, product back in stock, recent purchase. In Kanal, you set up the flow once and it applies to every customer concerned.

SMS or email: which one should I use?

They work together. Email carries long, illustrated content; SMS, limited to 160 characters, is for short and urgent messages: the end of a sale, a parcel out for delivery, a product back in stock. Kanal sends SMS, not email: it connects to your email tool, Klaviyo for example.

Does Kanal also send WhatsApp messages?

Yes. Kanal sends SMS and WhatsApp campaigns from the same app, with the same segments and the same report. WhatsApp adds images, buttons and replies; SMS reaches every phone, with no app to install.
